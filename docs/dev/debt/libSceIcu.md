# libSceIcu technical debt

- The 92 `sceIcuUnknown_*_nid_no_patch` exports in [Export.cpp](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Export.cpp) are present in the PS5 3.20 export inventory from [PS5-3.20_Libs](https://github.com/DNNDHH/PS5-3.20_Libs). Their prototypes and behavior are unverified. Each uses the no-parameter import-resolution convention and throws through `NotImplemented_nid_no_patch`; these declarations make no ABI or behavior claim.
- [Cleanup](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Configuration.cpp) is unverified on a console. `u_cleanup` follows ICU 78.1 [ucln_cmn.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/049e0d6a420629ac7db77256987d083a563287b5/icu4c/source/common/ucln_cmn.cpp) for the state this module keeps: it clears the functions stored by `u_setMemoryFunctions` and resets the data and time zone files directories. Pointers returned earlier by the directory getters are invalidated, as in ICU. ICU's tracing and per-library cleanup chains have no counterpart here. The NID comes from the 3.20 inventory and its presence on current firmware is unproven (see ICU export availability). Closes when: compared with the console.

The default is empty; `ICU_TIMEZONE_FILES_DIR` and the build-time default are not read, and nothing in this module reads time zone files. A null `UErrorCode` throws where ICU dereferences it. The NIDs come from the 3.20 inventory and their presence on current firmware is unproven (see ICU export availability). Closes when: compared with the console.

### Functional

- [C string helpers](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/CString.cpp) have no console ABI or behavior verification. Their names/NIDs are in the PS5 3.20 inventory; signatures and behavior follow ICU 78.1's [cstring.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/049e0d6a420629ac7db77256987d083a563287b5/icu4c/source/common/cstring.cpp), unchanged since 65.1 apart from casts. Formatting accepts radices 2–16; null buffers or other radices throw an invalid-argument exception, whereas ICU dereferences the buffer and only asserts the radix. Signed minima and outputs exceeding ICU's 30-byte temporary use unsigned arithmetic and caller storage without reproducing upstream undefined behavior. Parsing uses guest LP64 `strtoul` and keeps the low 32 bits; a null source throws. Cross-firmware availability, native invalid-input behavior and the undefined upstream cases remain unverified.

- [Escape decoding](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Unescape.cpp) has no behavior or ABI verification on console hardware. It follows ICU 78.1's [ustring.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/049e0d6a420629ac7db77256987d083a563287b5/icu4c/source/common/ustring.cpp), including rejection of eight-digit braced hexadecimal escapes although its public header advertises up to eight digits. Null source or offset pointers, null callbacks with an in-range offset, negative capacity with a nonnull destination and source lengths exceeding `INT32_MAX` throw. Null-destination preflight ignores capacity. Closes when: compared with the console, including callback ABI, byte conversion, capacity limits and braced hexadecimal parsing.

- [Invariant character and version helpers](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Version.cpp) are unverified on a console. `u_charsToUChars`, `u_UCharsToChars`, `u_versionFromString`, `u_versionFromUString` and `u_versionToString` follow ICU 78.1 [uinvchar.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/release-78.1/icu4c/source/common/uinvchar.cpp) and [putil.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/release-78.1/icu4c/source/common/putil.cpp), unchanged since 65.1. Firmware 13.42 reports ICU 78.1 and exports none of them ([#11013](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/11013#issuecomment-6400470698)); the [3.20 table](https://github.com/DNNDHH/PS5-3.20_Libs/blob/b2a77c09cd71c01f34c790cfbe98c751a13262ad/libSceIcu.c) has all five. Version parts use the guest `strtoul`, truncated to eight bits; `u_versionToString` keeps ICU's dropped zero tens digit (105 becomes `15`). A null buffer with a positive conversion length throws; ICU dereferences it. Closes when: compared with the console.
