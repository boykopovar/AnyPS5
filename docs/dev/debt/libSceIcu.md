# libSceIcu technical debt

### Functional

- [Invariant character and version helpers](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Version.cpp) are unverified on a console. `u_charsToUChars`, `u_UCharsToChars`, `u_versionFromString`, `u_versionFromUString` and `u_versionToString` follow ICU 78.1 [uinvchar.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/release-78.1/icu4c/source/common/uinvchar.cpp) and [putil.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/release-78.1/icu4c/source/common/putil.cpp), unchanged since 65.1. Firmware 13.42 reports ICU 78.1 and exports none of them ([#11013](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/11013#issuecomment-6400470698)); the [3.20 table](https://github.com/DNNDHH/PS5-3.20_Libs/blob/b2a77c09cd71c01f34c790cfbe98c751a13262ad/libSceIcu.c) has all five. Version parts use the guest `strtoul`, truncated to eight bits; `u_versionToString` keeps ICU's dropped zero tens digit (105 becomes `15`). A null buffer with a positive conversion length throws; ICU dereferences it. Closes when: compared with the console.
- [Numeric platform helpers](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Numeric.cpp) are unverified on a console. The 24 `uprv_` IEEE 754, rounding, extreme, overflow and pointer-limit exports follow ICU 78.1 [putil.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/release-78.1/icu4c/source/common/putil.cpp) and [umath.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/release-78.1/icu4c/source/common/umath.cpp), unchanged since 65.1, assuming the default `IEEE_754` and `U_MAX_PTR` configuration. They are in the 3.20 table, but firmware 13.42 exports none of them ([#11013](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/11013#issuecomment-6401310351)). `floor`, `ceil`, `fabs`, `modf`, `fmod`, `pow` and `log` are the guest libc's, so their precision is libc's. ICU's quirks are kept: `uprv_trunc` of negative infinity is positive infinity, NaN results are the canonical quiet NaN, `uprv_fmax(0.0, -0.0)` is `-0.0`, and `uprv_round` floors `x + 0.5`. A null `uprv_modf` integral pointer or overflow result pointer throws; ICU dereferences it. Closes when: compared with the console.
