# libSceIcu technical debt

### Unknown function info

- [ICU export availability](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Unescape.cpp) across firmware versions remains unproven. The [PS5 3.20 inventory](https://github.com/DNNDHH/PS5-3.20_Libs/blob/b2a77c09cd71c01f34c790cfbe98c751a13262ad/libSceIcu.c) includes the implemented escape, invariant-character and version helpers. [BunnyFl’s firmware 13.42 report](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/10961#issuecomment-6400071863) records `u_getVersion` returning `78.1.0.0` under an emulator, but neither escape function is in the checked native `system_ex/common_ex/lib/libSceIcu.sprx` export table. Only 104 of the 661 inventory NIDs appear among that module’s 299 exports. The exports for compatibility with older firmware are retained; availability and routing across firmware versions remain unproven.

### Break iterator factories

- [Break iterator factories](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Export.cpp) - Four 3.20 callable NIDs are the ICU 68 `BreakIterator::createCharacterInstance`, `createWordInstance`, `createLineInstance` and `createSentenceInstance(const Locale&, UErrorCode&)`: their `icu_68` mangled names hash to these NIDs, and the selector each wrapper passes is the `UBreakIteratorType` that ICU 68 passes to `BreakIterator::createInstance`. They are declared as throwing placeholders; their prototypes are not verified and break iteration needs ICU rule data that no host library provides. The four selector wrappers have identical instruction bytes in the 3.20 and later version-labelled modules, and corresponding consumer switch cases call them. Their NIDs and internal layouts differ, and the later NIDs match no `icu_60` to `icu_79` name of these functions; no forwarding or binary compatibility is claimed. Later image version metadata does not authenticate a retail firmware release.

A name match does not show that the console function has ICU 68's prototype; 939 of them are implemented from the ICU 68.2 sources in the entries below, and the others throw through `NotImplemented_nid_no_patch`. The other 656 keep placeholder names; 652 of them match no ICU 68.2 symbol. These NIDs do not appear in the later version-labelled module inventory; no cross-version identity or compatibility is claimed.

The binary reader retains ICU's format checks rather than validating every index in advance; callers retain ownership of serialized storage. `ucptrie_swap` remains a stub pending the data swapper implementation. Closes when: compared with the console.

- [Character iterators](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/CharacterIterator.cpp) have no console ABI or behavior verification. Assignment, post-increment dispatch, UTF-16 iteration, movement, text replacement, hashing and class IDs follow ICU 68.2 `chariter.cpp` and `uchriter.cpp` with the LP64 layouts and virtual slots of `unicode/chariter.h`. Null pointers when accessed, negative text indices and overflowing position or hash arithmetic throw `std::invalid_argument` instead of undefined behavior. The ForwardCharacterIterator, CharacterIterator and UCharCharacterIterator constructors and destructors, UCharCharacterIterator `clone`, equality and `getText` are not implemented and throw. Closes when: compared with the console.

- [UCharsTrie](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/UCharsTrie.cpp) has no console ABI or behavior verification. LP64 reader, pointer-wrapper and sink layouts and guest callbacks are checked. Null pointers when accessed and overflowing branch-length arithmetic throw `std::invalid_argument` instead of undefined behavior. Serialized arrays carry no bounds; callers must supply complete valid ICU trie data. No assigned UCharsTrie exports remain stubs. Closes when: compared with the console.

- [BytesTrie](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/BytesTrie.cpp) has no console ABI or behavior verification. - [Edits](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Edits.cpp) has no console ABI or behavior verification. Closes when: compared with the console.

- [UVector32](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Vector32.cpp) has no console ABI or behavior verification. Constructors and destructors remain stubs because guest vtable and RTTI construction and destruction are not implemented. Closes when: compared with the console.

- [UVector and UStack](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Vector.cpp) have no console ABI or behavior verification. Storage, callbacks, comparisons, mutation and sorting follow ICU 68.2 `uvector.cpp`, `ustack.cpp` and `uarrsort.cpp`, including failure-status mutation, the distinct `equals` callback arguments and deletion during stack pops. Constructors and destructors remain stubs because guest vtable and RTTI construction and destruction are not implemented; integer `indexOf` and integer `sortedInsert` wrappers remain stubs because upstream passes unions with uninitialized upper bytes to guest callbacks. Closes when: compared with the console.

- [UTF-8 macro helpers](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Utf8.cpp) have no console ABI or behavior verification. Their names and NIDs are in the [PS5 3.20 inventory](https://github.com/DNNDHH/PS5-3.20_Libs/blob/b2a77c09cd71c01f34c790cfbe98c751a13262ad/libSceIcu.c); behavior follows ICU 78.1 [utf_impl.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/049e0d6a420629ac7db77256987d083a563287b5/icu4c/source/common/utf_impl.cpp). These are macro bodies, not complete UTF-8 operations: append encodes ASCII with two bytes, and null-error-pointer failures write the legacy length-dependent error characters. Null index pointers, negative positions, reversed bounds, overflowing index or capacity arithmetic, and null buffers when accessed throw instead of reproducing undefined behavior. Negative forward lengths denote NUL-terminated input. Cross-firmware export availability and native behavior outside the macros' preconditions remain unverified.

- [Space classification](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Space.cpp) is unverified on a console. `u_isspace`, `u_isblank`, `u_isWhitespace`, `u_isJavaSpaceChar` and `u_isUWhiteSpace` follow ICU 78.1 [uchar.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/049e0d6a420629ac7db77256987d083a563287b5/icu4c/source/common/uchar.cpp#L196-L244) over fixed Zs, Zl, Zp and White_Space sets instead of ICU's property data; the sets are unchanged from ICU 65.1 to 78.1. Closes when: compared with the console.

- [C string helpers](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/CString.cpp) have no console ABI or behavior verification. Their names/NIDs are in the PS5 3.20 inventory; signatures and behavior follow ICU 78.1's [cstring.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/049e0d6a420629ac7db77256987d083a563287b5/icu4c/source/common/cstring.cpp), unchanged since 65.1 apart from casts. Formatting accepts radices 2–16; null buffers or other radices throw an invalid-argument exception, whereas ICU dereferences the buffer and only asserts the radix. Signed minima and outputs exceeding ICU's 30-byte temporary use unsigned arithmetic and caller storage without reproducing upstream undefined behavior. Parsing uses guest LP64 `strtoul` and keeps the low 32 bits; a null source throws. Cross-firmware availability, native invalid-input behavior and the undefined upstream cases remain unverified.

- [Converter STOP/SKIP callbacks](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/ConverterCallbacks.cpp) have no console ABI or behavior verification. Signatures and decisions follow ICU 78.1's [ucnv_err.h](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/049e0d6a420629ac7db77256987d083a563287b5/icu4c/source/common/unicode/ucnv_err.h) and [ucnv_err.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/049e0d6a420629ac7db77256987d083a563287b5/icu4c/source/common/ucnv_err.cpp). The callback-specific default-ignorable ranges include U+180F, which ICU 65.1 excludes; firmware-dependent selection is not implemented. Reasons outside the upstream enum's 0–7 value range and a null error pointer on a path that writes it throw `invalid_argument`; native behavior there is undefined. Untouched pointers remain unread, including the error pointer on no-write paths. Converter argument structures stay opaque because these callbacks do not access them; converter execution and other callbacks remain unavailable.

- [Escape decoding](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Unescape.cpp) has no behavior or ABI verification on console hardware. It follows ICU 78.1's [ustring.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/049e0d6a420629ac7db77256987d083a563287b5/icu4c/source/common/ustring.cpp), including rejection of eight-digit braced hexadecimal escapes although its public header advertises up to eight digits. Null source or offset pointers and null callbacks with an in-range offset, which ICU dereferences, throw `std::invalid_argument`. A negative capacity writes nothing, as in ICU; source lengths exceeding `INT32_MAX` and a capacity that overflows ICU's signed `destCapacity - i` throw. Null-destination preflight ignores capacity. Closes when: compared with the console, including callback ABI, byte conversion, capacity limits and braced hexadecimal parsing.

- [Invariant character and version helpers](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Version.cpp) are unverified on a console. `u_charsToUChars`, `u_UCharsToChars`, `u_versionFromString`, `u_versionFromUString` and `u_versionToString` follow ICU 78.1 [uinvchar.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/release-78.1/icu4c/source/common/uinvchar.cpp) and [putil.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/release-78.1/icu4c/source/common/putil.cpp), unchanged since 65.1. Version parts use the guest `strtoul`, truncated to eight bits; `u_versionToString` keeps ICU's dropped zero tens digit (105 becomes `15`). A null buffer with a positive conversion length throws; ICU dereferences it. Closes when: compared with the console.

- [Iterator comparison and Unicode version](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/CompareIter.cpp) are unverified on a console. `u_strCompareIter` and `u_getUnicodeVersion` follow ICU 78.1 [ustring.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/049e0d6a4206/icu4c/source/common/ustring.cpp#L828-L882) and [uchar.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/049e0d6a4206/icu4c/source/common/uchar.cpp#L484-L489); the version is 17.0, the Unicode data of 78.1, whatever firmware is emulated. - [Default-converter string copy helpers](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Invariant.cpp) `u_austrcpy` and `u_austrncpy` are unverified on a console and throw `NotImplemented_nid_no_patch` on code units above `0x7f`. Closes when: compared with the console, including the default converter for non-ASCII units.
