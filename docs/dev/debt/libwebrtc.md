- [absl::numbers_internal::FastIntToBuffer(int, char*)](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/Export.cpp) (libwebrtc) - follows the abseil both branches pin, [65ef38d368](https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/third_party/+/65ef38d36813d1ad9d4cbf8a18b32ac3691f418b/abseil-cpp/absl/strings/numbers.cc) and [d212a71416](https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/third_party/+/d212a71416ad7c6ceca04d6d27dfdd84fd00c04c/abseil-cpp/absl/strings/numbers.cc): the decimal text and a NUL, returning a pointer to the NUL. For magnitudes from 10 to 99999999 its 8-byte store also zeroes the bytes after the digits up to 8 from the first digit. A null buffer, which abseil writes through, throws. Closes when: compared with the console.
- [rtc::TimeMillis and rtc::TimeMicros](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/Export.cpp) (libwebrtc) - follow `TimeNanos` and the POSIX `SystemTimeNanos` in [time_utils.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/rtc_base/time_utils.cc) and [system_time.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/rtc_base/system_time.cc), the same at both branches: the guest `CLOCK_MONOTONIC` in nanoseconds, divided down. Whether the module is built with `WEBRTC_EXCLUDE_SYSTEM_TIME` and a console clock of its own is unknown. No export sets a clock for testing, so the `g_clock` path is left out. A failing `clock_gettime` throws. Closes when: compared with the console.
- [rtc::ExpFilter::Reset and Apply](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/ExpFilter.cpp) (libwebrtc) - adapted from [exp_filter.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/rtc_base/numerics/exp_filter.cc), with its WebRTC copyright notice and BSD licence at the top of the file, on the 12-byte `alpha_`, `filtered_` and `max_` layout of `exp_filter.h`. -1 marks both an unset output and no maximum, so an output that lands on exactly -1 restarts the filter on the next sample, as upstream does. The last bit of a result can differ if the console build fuses the multiply-adds. A null filter throws. Closes when: compared with the console.
- [webrtc::rtcp::CommonHeader::Parse](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/RtcpCommonHeader.cpp) (libwebrtc) - adapted from [common_header.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/modules/rtp_rtcp/source/rtcp_packet/common_header.cc), with its WebRTC copyright notice and BSD licence at the top of the file, on the 16-byte layout of `common_header.h`: packet type, count or format and padding size at 0 to 2, payload size at 4 and payload pointer at 8. Its checks run in the same order, so a short buffer or a wrong version leaves the header untouched and the later failures leave the fields already parsed. The `RTC_LOG(LS_WARNING)` message each failure logs upstream is not emitted. A null header, or a null buffer of 4 bytes or more, which upstream reads, throws. Closes when: the warnings are logged through libwebrtc's logging and the parse is compared with the console.
- [WebRtcOpus encoder functions](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/Opus.cpp) (libwebrtc) - the 16 `WebRtcOpus_*` encoder exports are adapted from [opus_interface.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/modules/audio_coding/codecs/opus/opus_interface.cc), with its WebRTC copyright notice and BSD licence at the top of the file. They encode through the bundled libopus 1.6.1, not WebRTC's pinned copy, so packet bytes can differ. Signatures match at both revisions; M127 only adds a DTX change behind the `WebRTC-Audio-OpusAvoidNoisePumpingDuringDtx` field trial, which stays off because `InitFieldTrialsFromString` throws. Frames over 60 ms, up to 120 ms, throw: they depend on `WEBRTC_OPUS_SUPPORT_120MS_PTIME`. A null encoder or buffer in `Encode`, which upstream reads, throws. Closes when: the build flag is known and packets are compared with the console.
