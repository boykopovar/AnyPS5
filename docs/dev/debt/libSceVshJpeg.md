# libSceVshJpeg technical debt

### Known export names; ABI prototypes unverified

- [libSceVshJpeg](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceVshJpeg/CMakeLists.txt) defines 10 of the 25 exports of the 13.60 list; the other 15 (14 decompression exports and `jpeg_std_error`) are defined by libSceNKWebKitRequirements and not copied here. That list matches the 25 names the 13.60 export list of [PS5-13.60-Function-Stub-Library](https://github.com/xdCloudy/PS5-13.60-Function-Stub-Library/blob/bfec40deb4c47a17d7603ee2bc79aa6862c0c56b/data/firmware-13.60/libscevshjpeg-f4d3f6e0b9.jsonl) by name; the NIDs come from the names. Prototypes are the public libjpeg 6b ones (`JPEG_LIB_VERSION` 62) that [libSceNKWebKitRequirements](libSceNKWebKitRequirements.md) already assumes for its decompression exports; the console's prototypes are not confirmed. Closes when: compared with the console.

### Functional

- [libjpeg compression exports](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceVshJpeg/Compress.cpp) have no console verification. `jpeg_CreateCompress`, `jpeg_set_defaults`, `jpeg_set_quality`, `jpeg_start_compress`, `jpeg_write_scanlines`, `jpeg_finish_compress` and `jpeg_destroy_compress` run libjpeg-turbo from `3rdparty/libjpeg-turbo`; the console's libjpeg version is unknown. Closes when: compared with the console.
- [Compression guest structures](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceVshJpeg/include/GuestJpegCompress.hpp): the 520-byte `jpeg_compress_struct` and `jpeg_destination_mgr` are LP64 mirrors of libjpeg 6b (`JPEG_LIB_VERSION` 62), the assumption libSceNKWebKitRequirements makes for its 632-byte decompression mirror, copied around each call and guest destination callback. A non-null guest `progress` throws through `NotImplemented_nid_no_patch`. The guest `mem` forwards `alloc_small`, `alloc_large`, `alloc_sarray`, `alloc_barray` and `free_pool` to libjpeg-turbo's pool; the virtual-array functions and `self_destruct` throw. `comp_info`, the tables and `scan_info` are copied between the guest structure and libjpeg-turbo's layout on each call and dangle after `jpeg_destroy_compress`, which clears `mem`. Closes when: compared with the console.
- The 15 exports missing from [this library](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceVshJpeg/Export.cpp) are defined only by [libSceNKWebKitRequirements](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceNKWebKitRequirements/Jpeg.cpp), as the duplicate-export check requires. The library links libSceNKWebKitRequirements, so loading it loads that library, and a Linux `dlopen` test resolves them through libSceVshJpeg. Not checked on Windows or macOS (the Windows entry stub searches `libs` itself) and not with a title. Their debt entry in [libSceNKWebKitRequirements](libSceNKWebKitRequirements.md) applies. The compression exports need `jpeg_std_error` from there.
- [jpeg_abort, jpeg_stdio_dest and jpeg_stdio_src](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceVshJpeg/Export.cpp) throw through `NotImplemented_nid_no_patch`. The stdio pair needs a guest `FILE`, which has no host counterpart, and `jpeg_abort` has to reach the object tables of the compression adapter and of any decompression adapter.
