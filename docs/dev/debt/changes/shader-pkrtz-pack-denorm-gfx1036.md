# shader

## change [v_cvt_pkrtz_f16_f32 and v_pack_b32_f16 denormals]
- [v_cvt_pkrtz_f16_f32 and v_pack_b32_f16 denormals](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/ConvertInstructions.cpp) follow the `FLOAT_MODE` f16 denormal field: `v_cvt_pkrtz_f16_f32` flushes each denormal half result to its signed zero when bit 1 is clear (0 and 1), and `v_pack_b32_f16` flushes each denormal half input to its signed zero when bit 0 is clear (0 and 2). Measured on RDNA2 (gfx1031, Radeon RX 6700 XT, AMD Windows driver 32.0.21045.5002 through HIP) at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1 with denorm16 0..3 and f32 denormals kept, over 256 pairs of 16 f32 values around the f16 denormal range for `v_cvt_pkrtz_f16_f32` and 256 pairs of 16 halves (zeros, denormals, minimum normals, ±1, the largest finite value, infinities and quiet and signaling NaNs) for `v_pack_b32_f16`. Shaders without a known float mode keep the previous lowering. The PS5's gfx1013 is not measured. Corroborated on gfx1036 (Windows driver 31.0.22044.1, HIP) over 2 pairs each, denorm32 0 and 3, denorm16 0..3.
