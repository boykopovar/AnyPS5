## change over 2,096 results

- [Signaling NaN inputs](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/FloatInstructions.cpp) of the f32 and f16 unary instructions (`v_sqrt/rcp/rsq/log/exp/sin/cos/fract/floor/ceil/trunc/rndne/rcp_iflag/frexp_mant`), `v_ldexp_f32/f16`, `v_frexp_mant_f16`, `v_div_fixup_f32/f16` and `v_mullit_f32` are returned quieted only with IEEE=1 and unchanged with IEEE=0, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1035) in both modes, and so are those of the f64 forms (`v_sqrt/rcp/rsq/fract/floor/ceil/trunc/rndne/frexp_mant/ldexp/div_fixup_f64`) and `v_cvt_f64_f32`, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036) in both modes; shaders without a known float mode follow IEEE=0. `v_pack_b32_f16` quiets each signaling NaN half by the same rule, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036) in both modes. A signaling NaN result of `v_cubesc/cubetc/cubema_f32` is quieted by the same rule and keeps the sign the instruction gives it, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036) in both modes. `v_add/sub/subrev/mul/fma/fmac/fmamk/fmaak/mul_legacy_f32` and `v_fma_mix_f32` choose their NaN result on the bits, measured over 1000 operand triples in both modes, and for `v_fma_mix_f32` with f16 sources over 2,096 results in both modes on RDNA2 (gfx1031, AMD Windows driver 32.0.21045.16010 through HIP), with an f16 NaN source widened on its bits (its sign, 0x7f800000 and its mantissa shifted left by 13) before its modifiers: the first NaN source in operand order after its modifiers (the subtrahend negated, as the hardware adds its negation), quieted only with IEEE=1, or the default NaN 0xffc00000 for an invalid operation; in the fma forms an invalid product (inf * 0) gives the default NaN even when src2 is a NaN, and in the legacy forms a zero factor makes the product 0 even when the other factor is a NaN (a NaN src2 still wins). `v_mad_legacy_f32` and `v_mac_legacy_f32` follow the same rule with their separately rounded product, as measured on gfx1010 (#1281, #1290): a NaN source quieted only with IEEE=1, inf - inf giving 0xffc00000, the zero rule hiding a NaN factor and a NaN src2 winning. `v_add_f64`, `v_mul_f64`, `v_fma_f64` and `v_div_fmas_f64` follow the same rule with the default NaN 0xfff8000000000000, an inf * 0 product winning over a NaN src2, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1031) in both modes. `v_add/sub/subrev/mul_f16` follow the same rule with the default NaN 0xfe00 on the native and the f32-emulated f16 paths, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036) at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1 over 12 operand pairs of quiet and signaling NaNs, infinities and zeros in the VOP2 and VOP3 forms. The f16 fma forms and the separately rounded mad/mac forms (not measurable on RDNA2) follow the same rule on hardware but their NaN bits are left to the driver, except that an invalid operation of `v_fma_f16`, `v_fmac_f16`, `v_fmamk_f16` and `v_fmaak_f16` (an inf * 0 product, also with a NaN src2, or a NaN from sources that are not NaN) gives the default NaN 0xfe00, which NVIDIA does not return on its own. Measured on RDNA2 (gfx1031, Radeon RX 6700 XT, AMD Windows driver 32.0.21045.5002 through HIP) at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1 with f16 and f32 denormals kept and flushed, over six inf * 0 products with eight NaN src2 values in every f16 fma form (#7074); the AMD Windows driver 32.0.21042.62 on an RDNA2 iGPU (Ryzen 5 7600) instead returns src2 quieted through Vulkan in the f16 fma forms. `v_cvt_f16_f32`, `v_cvt_f32_f64`, `v_dot2_f32_f16`, `v_fma_mixlo_f16` and `v_fma_mixhi_f16` always quiet and `v_div_scale_f32` never does: the f32 signaling NaN 0x7f812345 as src0 or src2 gives 0x7e09 from mixlo and mixhi at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1 on RDNA2 (gfx1036), where `v_fma_mix_f32` gives 0x7f812345 at IEEE=0 and 0x7fc12345 at IEEE=1. The payload of an f16 NaN source of mixlo and mixhi is left to the driver's f16 to f32 conversion, which NVIDIA replaces.
