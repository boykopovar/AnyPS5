# libSceAgcDriver

## change [UINT color targets]

- [UINT color targets](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Graphics/src/State.cpp) (libSceAgcDriver) - whether the console's CB blends into integer targets is not measured, and what a raw 32-bit export into an 8- or 16-bit UINT channel stores is measured on Navi21 only (#11513), not on the console: `32_R` keeps the word's low bits, `32_GR` and `32_ABGR` clamp it as an unsigned integer, and `32_AR` gave no consistent result there, so it is passed unchanged and the attachment's conversion, which Vulkan leaves undefined, decides. Here a raw 32-bit export (`32_R`, `32_GR`, `32_AR`, `32_ABGR`) into a UINT target is passed as the exported bits through the `Uint` export packing, masked (`32_R`, the `UintMask8` and `UintMask16` packings) or clamped (`32_GR` and `32_ABGR`, `UintClamp8` and `UintClamp16`) to an 8- or 16-bit channel, and the target's blend is dropped. A `2_10_10_10` UINT target with `COMP_SWAP` 0 is `A2B10G10R10_UINT`, and its exports take the `UintMask10_10_10_2` and `UintClamp10_10_10_2` packings, which mask or clamp the first three channels to 10 bits and the fourth to 2 bits. What Navi21 or the console stores for a word wider than a 10- or 2-bit channel is not measured, so these follow the 8- and 16-bit measurement; `COMP_SWAP` 1 (`A2R10G10B10_UINT`) throws; the execution test draws `32_R` into `R32_UINT`, `32_GR` into `R32G32_UINT` and `32_AR` and `32_ABGR` into `R32G32B32A32_UINT` (tiled and linear) and reads every written channel back bit for bit, draws words wider than the channel into 8- and 16-bit UINT targets, and draws through each masking and clamping packing into `R32_UINT` and `R32G32B32A32_UINT`; it draws in-range words, wider words and halves into `A2B10G10R10_UINT` (tiled and linear) and reads each 10- and 2-bit field back. On lavapipe (Mesa, LLVM 20) a constant export of exactly 1.0 in the first channel and -0.0 in the second stores +0.0 in the second although the module declares `SignedZeroInfNanPreserve`, so the test leaves that pair out. The blend is dropped since Vulkan's framebuffer chapter says blending is only defined for floating-point, UNORM, SNORM and sRGB formats and Metal refuses both the float output and the blend on integer attachments (#9783). An `FP16_ABGR` export into a UINT target stores each half widened to a 32-bit float (a NaN with its quiet bit set) and read as an unsigned integer, clamped to the channel, so an 8- or 16-bit channel holds 0 for +0.0 and its maximum for any other half; two Navi21 GPUs (RDNA2) store this with RADV forced to `FP16_ABGR` (#11513), the console is not measured. The shader widens the half bits with integer operations and the `UintClamp8` and `UintClamp16` packings clamp them; the execution test draws halves read at run time into 32-, 16- and 8-bit UINT targets, and through each clamping packing into `R32_UINT`. An uncompressed `FP16_ABGR` export into a UINT target keeps its 32-bit words without a conversion to half, which is not measured. `UNORM16_ABGR`, `SNORM16_ABGR` and `SINT16_ABGR` exports into UINT targets throw.
