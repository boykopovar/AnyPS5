# libwebrtc

## add Public source implementations

- [webrtc::AudioDecoderOpusImpl](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/AudioDecoderOpusImpl.cpp) (libwebrtc) - console layout, vtable and allocator are unverified on the console. Follows [audio_decoder_opus.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/modules/audio_coding/codecs/opus/audio_decoder_opus.cc) and [opus_interface.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/modules/audio_coding/codecs/opus/opus_interface.cc) for the M134 three-argument `AudioDecoderOpusImpl(const FieldTrialsView&, size_t, int)` constructor and destructor on the 32-byte layout (`vtable` at 0, `dec_state_` at 8, `channels_` at 16, `sample_rate_hz_` at 24, `generate_plc_` at 28). Reads `"WebRTC-Audio-OpusGeneratePlc"` via guest `FieldTrialsView::Lookup` at virtual slot 2 (15-byte SSO or heap string freed through guest `operator delete`) and decodes through bundled `3rdparty/opus` rather than WebRTC's pinned copy. Populates the 15-slot `AudioDecoder` vtable (reusing base `HasDecodePlc`, `DecodePlc` and `ErrorCode` exports and returning `OpusFrame` instances allocated with guest `new` in `ParsePayload`, where `OpusFrame` assumes the 24-byte `std::optional<DecodeResult>` hidden-result ABI). Null or inaccessible decoders, field trials, buffers, invalid channel counts (not 1 or 2) or sample rates (not 16000 or 48000), and sample-rate mismatches throw instead of relying on debug-only checks. Closes when: compared with the console.
