# shader

## change [v_mad_legacy_f32 and v_mac_legacy_f32]

- [v_mad_legacy_f32 and v_mac_legacy_f32](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/FloatInstructions.cpp) (VOP3 0x140, VOP2 0x06) round the product to f32 before the add, give a +0 product for a zero factor, and flush their f32 denormal sources, their product and their result to a zero of the same sign in every f32 denormal mode, the product when it is still below 2^-126 after rounding to 24 bits with an unbounded exponent, so a product just below 2^-126 that rounds to it is kept (measured on gfx1010 over 1500 such products in all four round modes, and over 45504 rows of both instructions at round to nearest even in the four f32 denormal modes at IEEE 0 and 1 with no mismatch), an exception to the `FLOAT_MODE` model in [f32DenormalFlushFor](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/VectorInstructions.cpp). Measured on gfx1010 (RX 5700 XT) with `tools/hw-oracle/template.s`, assembled by the AMD Windows driver's `amd_comgr_2.dll` and launched through its `amdhip64_6.dll` instead of `oracle.c` and ROCr, over the 32 rows of the five `FmaLegacy.cpp` and the four `MacLegacy.cpp` encodings: identical in wave32 and wave64 at IEEE 0 and 1 with f32 denormal modes 0 to 3, except that the two signaling NaN rows are quieted only with IEEE=1 (#1281, #1290). A sum of two zeros takes its sign on the bits (+0 unless both are -0), as lavapipe returned -0 for +0 + -0. The PS5's gfx1013 is not measured; LLVM decodes the encoding as `v_mad_legacy_f32` there, as on gfx1010. RDNA2 differs: on gfx1031 (RX 6700 XT, measured by pandather in #1281) the encoding is `v_fma_legacy_f32` and fused, with the same zero rule and denormal-source flush, so an RDNA2 measurement is not evidence for the console.
