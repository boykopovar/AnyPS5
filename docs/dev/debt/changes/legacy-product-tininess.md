# shader

## change [v_mad_legacy_f32 and v_mac_legacy_f32]

- [v_mad_legacy_f32 and v_mac_legacy_f32](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/FloatInstructions.cpp) (VOP3 0x140, VOP2 0x06) round the product to f32 before the add, give a +0 product for a zero factor, and flush their f32 denormal sources, their product and their result to a zero of the same sign in every f32 denormal mode, the product when it is still below 2^-126 after rounding to 24 bits with an unbounded exponent, so a product just below 2^-126 that rounds to it is kept (measured on gfx1010 over 1500 such products in all four round modes, and over 45504 rows of both instructions at round to nearest even in the four f32 denormal modes at IEEE 0 and 1 with no mismatch), an exception to the `FLOAT_MODE` model in [f32DenormalFlushFor](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/VectorInstructions.cpp). Measured on gfx1010 (RX 5700 XT) with `tools/hw-oracle/template.s`, assembled by the AMD Windows driver's `amd_comgr_2.dll` and launched through its `amdhip64_6.dll` instead of `oracle.c` and ROCr, over the 32 rows of the five `FmaLegacy.cpp` and the four `MacLegacy.cpp` encodings: identical in wave32 and wave64 at IEEE 0 and 1 with f32 denormal modes 0 to 3, except that the two signaling NaN rows are quieted only with IEEE=1 (#1281, #1290). A sum of two zeros takes its sign on the bits (+0 unless both are -0), as lavapipe returned -0 for +0 + -0. The PS5's gfx1013 is not measured; LLVM decodes the encoding as `v_mad_legacy_f32` there, as on gfx1010. RDNA2 differs: on gfx1031 (RX 6700 XT, measured by pandather in #1281) the encoding is `v_fma_legacy_f32` and fused, with the same zero rule and denormal-source flush, so an RDNA2 measurement is not evidence for the console.

## change A product whose scaled check is exactly 2^-62 is emitted as 2^-126 of its sign

- [f32 denormals](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/TranslationContext/OperandAccess.cpp) follow the shader's `FLOAT_MODE` f32 denormal field (bits 5:4) when it flushes them (0, and shaders without a known float mode, as in the 0xc0 most titles use), measured on RDNA2 (gfx1035) over 1444 rows per instruction at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1. `v_add/sub/subrev/mul/mul_legacy/mullit/fma/fmac/fmamk/fmaak/min/max/min3/max3/med3/fract/floor/ceil/frexp_mant/sin/cos_f32` flush their f32 inputs and their result to a zero of the same sign (`v_mullit_f32` measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036) over 6907 rows per mode at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1, so a positive denormal src2 counts as zero); the f32 compares, `v_cvt_i32/u32/rpi_i32/flr_i32_f32`, `v_frexp_exp_i32_f32`, `v_cvt_f64_f32`, `v_cvt_f16_f32`, `v_cvt_pkrtz_f16_f32`, `v_cvt_pknorm_i16/u16_f32` and `v_cvt_pk_u8_f32` flush their f32 inputs. `v_div_scale_f32` flushes all three inputs to signed zeros, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036) over 2744 operand triples per mode at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1 with f32 denormals flushed and kept; its scale-down behaviour and host denormal preservation remain incomplete. `v_div_fixup_f32` flushes its src1 and src2 and keeps a denormal src0 and result, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036) over 6859 rows per mode at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1. Tininess is detected after rounding, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036) for `v_mul_f32` and `v_mullit_f32` at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1: a product is flushed when its value rounded to 24 bits with an unbounded exponent is below 2^-126, so `0x3f7ffffe * 0x00800001` (2^-126 * (1 - 2^-46)) gives `0x00800000` and `0x3f7fffff * 0x00800000` (2^-126 * (1 - 2^-24)) gives zero. The check rounds in the f32 round mode of `FLOAT_MODE` (bits 1:0), measured in all four modes, while the products themselves are still rounded to nearest even. This applies to `v_mul_f32`, `v_mul_legacy_f32`, `v_mullit_f32`, and to `v_fma_f32`, `v_fmac_f32`, `v_fmamk_f32`, `v_fmaak_f32` and `v_fma_mix_f32` when the addend is zero; with a nonzero addend the fused result is kept, so an exact result just below 2^-126 that rounds to it is not flushed. In the three directed modes the check is computed exactly from the significands. To nearest even (0, the mode of shaders without a known float mode) one factor is multiplied by 2^64, which is exact below 2^64, and the product with the other factor is compared with 2^-62. That multiply is a plain `OpFMul` like the product, and no f32 rounding mode is requested in the SPIR-V, so the check relies on the driver rounding f32 to nearest even by default, as the products do. A product whose scaled check is exactly 2^-62 is emitted as 2^-126 of its sign. `v_fma_mix_f32` follows this rule with mixed f16/f32 sources too, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036) with f16 in either multiplicand slot, low and high half selection, and an f32 or f16 zero addend: f32 `0x00801002` times f16 `0x3bff` gives `0x00800000` to nearest at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1, while the neighbours `0x00801001` and `0x00801003` give zero and `0x00800001`. The tiny-product boundary was also measured for both signs in all four f32 round modes at IEEE=0; Its f16 sources are flushed to a zero of their sign when bit 0 of the f16/f64 denormal field is clear (0 and 2), whatever the f32 field, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1031, Radeon RX 6700 XT, AMD Windows driver 32.0.21045.5002 through HIP) at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1 with f32 denormal modes 0 and 3 and f16 denormal modes 0 to 3, over 2744 triples of 14 halves with all three sources f16 and 1960 rows with an f32 multiplicand. `v_fma_mixlo_f16` and `v_fma_mixhi_f16` flush their f32 sources too and keep a product below 2^-126, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036). `v_mad_legacy_f32` and `v_mac_legacy_f32` flush their inputs, their product and their result in every mode, see its entry; their product is checked for tininess after rounding by the same rule, measured on gfx1010 (RX 5700 XT) in all four round modes, so one below 2^-126 that rounds to 2^-126 is kept. Denormal modes 1 and 2 follow the per-instruction entries below. Not covered, and not measurable on RDNA2 (no such opcodes) or not measured: `v_mac_f32`, `v_mad_f32`, `v_madak_f32` and `v_madmk_f32` (rounded separately), `v_dot2_f32_f16` and f32 interpolation keep the previous lowering, while `ds_add_f32` follows the field; `ds_min/max/cmpst_f32` and the image, buffer, global and flat `fmin/fmax/fcmpswap` flush both operands by the field, see the float atomics entries. With f32 denormals kept (3) the module requests `DenormPreserve` for f32 where the device offers it, and elsewhere the results depend on the driver's default denormal behaviour; `v_ldexp_f32` and the f32 compares are the exceptions. `v_cmp_X_f32` and `v_cmpx_X_f32` compare the operands as sign-magnitude integers with the NaN cases handled apart, so a denormal is not read as zero, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036) over 256 operand pairs per opcode at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1 with f32 denormals kept and flushed. `v_ldexp_f32` preserves subnormal inputs and rounds a subnormal result in the f32 round mode of `FLOAT_MODE`, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036) over 2163 rows in each of the four round modes at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1. An overflow rounds like any other result (infinity, or the largest finite value when the mode rounds that sign toward zero). With f32 denormals flushed it flushes a subnormal input and result, measured over the same rows in each round mode at IEEE=0 only. A device whose driver splits `Fma` into a multiply and an add flushes a product below 2^-126 before adding a non-zero addend in `v_fma_f32` and `v_fmac_f32`: `0x1fffffff * 0x1fffffff + 0x00800000` gives `0x00800000` instead of the fused `0x00ffffff` on RADV RENOIR (Mesa 26.2.4), whose compiled shader has `v_mad_f32` and no `v_fma_f32`. [F32DenormalFlush](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/tests/execution/F32DenormalFlush.cpp) accepts that sum on such a device.
