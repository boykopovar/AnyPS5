# libkernel

## change [_umtx_op]

- [_umtx_op](../../../core/libs/prx/libkernel/SyncOnAddress/src/SyncOnAddress.cpp) (libkernel) - legacy lock and `SHM` operations throw, as do robust PI or PP umutexes, unlocking a priority-protected one and misaligned objects; robust lists of threads not started by `scePthreadCreate` are ignored at exit. Umutex, condvar and rwlock operations match a PS5 payload (firmware 13.40): an owned mutex relocks without a count and one unlock frees it, a priority-protected one fails with `EINVAL`, a timed-out reader leaves `URWLOCK_READ_WAITERS` set, and owners are `scePthreadGetthreadid`. Priority inheritance is not applied; `CVWAIT_CHECK_UNPARKING` is ignored. Contention and wakeups otherwise follow FreeBSD 11's [kern_umtx.c](https://github.com/freebsd/freebsd-src/blob/5ed7eb0d97ba4436218e810f61bd059acba984c2/sys/kern/kern_umtx.c) (as do wait, wake, `SEM`, `SEM2`, `MUTEX_WAKE2` and robust ones). Operations 0 and 1 fail with `EOPNOTSUPP` and 7 on other objects with `EINVAL`, as measured, and 27 returns 0 here as it did on the console; what operation 27 does with valid arguments, and whether operations above 27 are refused, is unmeasured.
