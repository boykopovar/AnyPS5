# libkernel

## change [_umtx_op]

- [_umtx_op](../../../core/libs/prx/libkernel/SyncOnAddress/src/SyncOnAddress.cpp) (libkernel) - legacy lock, `MUTEX_WAKE2`, `SEM2` and `SHM` operations throw, as do robust PI or PP umutexes, unlocking a priority-protected one and misaligned objects; robust lists of threads not started by `scePthreadCreate` are ignored at exit. Umutex, condvar and rwlock operations match a PS5 payload (firmware 13.40): an owned mutex relocks without a count and one unlock frees it, a priority-protected one fails with `EINVAL`, a timed-out reader leaves `URWLOCK_READ_WAITERS` set, and owners are `scePthreadGetthreadid`. Priority inheritance and `CVWAIT_CHECK_UNPARKING` are ignored. Contention and wakeups otherwise follow FreeBSD 11's [kern_umtx.c](https://github.com/freebsd/freebsd-src/blob/stable/11/sys/kern/kern_umtx.c) (as do wait, wake, `SEM` and robust ones). Operations 0 and 1 fail with `EOPNOTSUPP` and 7 on other objects with `EINVAL`, as measured; 27 returns 0 on the console and `EINVAL` here.
