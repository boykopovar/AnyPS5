# shader

## change [v_add_f64 and v_mul_f64 denormals]

- [v_add_f64 and v_mul_f64 denormals](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/Float64Instructions.cpp) follow the `FLOAT_MODE` f16/f64 denormal and round fields as measured on RDNA2 (gfx1031) only; the PS5's gfx1013 is not measured. Inputs flush to a zero of their sign when bit 0 of the denormal field is clear (0 and 2). Rounding 1 to 3 rounds the exact result up, down or toward zero: an overflow gives infinity on the side the mode rounds toward and the largest finite f64 on the other, and a sum of opposite-signed equal values is -0 only when rounding down. When bit 1 is clear (0 and 1), a result whose exact value rounded in that mode to 53 significant bits with an unbounded exponent is below 2^-1022 flushes to a zero of its sign, so `0x2000000000000001 * 0x1ffffffffffffffe` (exactly 2^-1022 - 2^-1126) is kept at round to nearest and when rounding away from zero and flushed otherwise. The directed modes are computed with 64-bit integers ([f64_add_round and f64_mul_round](../../../core/shader/recompiler/SpirvBackend/src/SpirvAlu/SpirvAluEmitterFloat64.cpp)), which costs more than the native operation and needs the Int64 capability. Measured with [tools/hw-oracle](../HW_ORACLE.md) on an RX 6750 XT (AMD Windows driver 32.0.21045.16010, HIP) at IEEE=0 and 1, denorm16 0..3 and round16 0..3 over 4,356 pairs of 66 f64 values (zeros, denormals, minimum normals, values next to 1, to 2^-1022 and to the largest finite value, infinities and a NaN), with no mismatches; the f32 round field changes nothing. Round to nearest was first measured on an RX 6700 XT (driver 32.0.21045.5002) with neg, abs and clamp over 576 pairs. The `clamp` forms match in the directed modes over 96 pairs. An output modifier that overflows the result still gives infinity in the directed modes, which is not measured. Shaders without a known float mode keep the previous lowering. Corroborated on gfx1036 over 3 pairs at denorm16 0..3.
