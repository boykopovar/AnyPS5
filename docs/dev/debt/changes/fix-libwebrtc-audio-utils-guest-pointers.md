# libwebrtc

## change rtc::ExpFilter::Reset and Apply

- [rtc::ExpFilter::Reset and Apply](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/ExpFilter.cpp) (libwebrtc) - adapted from [exp_filter.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/rtc_base/numerics/exp_filter.cc), with its WebRTC copyright notice and BSD licence at the top of the file, on the 12-byte `alpha_`, `filtered_` and `max_` layout of `exp_filter.h`. Filters are copied through `GuestPtr` (`CopyIn`/`CopyOut`) and probed with `GuestWritable_nid_no_patch` before mutating guest memory. -1 marks both an unset output and no maximum, so an output that lands on exactly -1 restarts the filter on the next sample, as upstream does. The last bit of a result can differ if the console build fuses the multiply-adds. A null, inaccessible or non-writable filter throws. Closes when: compared with the console.

## change webrtc::RmsLevel::RmsLevel, ~RmsLevel, Analyze and Average

- [webrtc::RmsLevel::RmsLevel, ~RmsLevel, Analyze and Average](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/RmsLevel.cpp) (libwebrtc) - adapted from [rms_level.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/modules/audio_processing/rms_level.cc), with its copyright notice and BSD licence on top, on the 40-byte layout of `rms_level.h`: `sum_square_` at 0, `sample_count_` at 8, `max_sum_square_` at 16, and `block_size_`'s value and flag at 24 and 32. M127's `absl::optional` there is `std::optional`, as the pinned abseil sets [`ABSL_OPTION_USE_STD_OPTIONAL`](https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/third_party/+/65ef38d36813d1ad9d4cbf8a18b32ac3691f418b/abseil-cpp/absl/base/options.h#124) to 2, so both pins share the layout. `Analyze` gets the view's pointer and size after `this`, reads sample arrays through bounded 4096-sample `GuestBytes` chunks, and state objects go through `GuestPtr`/`GuestWritable_nid_no_patch` before updating guest memory. The destructor validates readability through `GuestPtr` and does nothing, as upstream's is defaulted; a null, inaccessible or non-writable object or sample slice throws. The console's `log10` may round a level differently. Not compared with the console.

## change webrtc::AudioCodecInfo five-argument constructor

- [webrtc::AudioCodecInfo five-argument constructor](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/AudioCodecInfo.cpp) (libwebrtc) - the console layout and behaviour remain unverified. Adapted from [audio_format.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/api/audio_codecs/audio_format.cc), retaining its WebRTC copyright notice and BSD licence. The constructor and `AudioCodecInfo` struct in [audio_format.h](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/api/audio_codecs/audio_format.h) are identical at M127 and M134; Sony's exact revision is unknown. `GuestWritable_nid_no_patch` and `GuestPtr::CopyOut` validate and write the struct without direct guest pointer dereferences; a null, inaccessible or non-writable object throws `std::invalid_argument`; arguments outside upstream's debug-only `RTC_DCHECK` bounds (positive sample rate and channel count, nonnegative minimum bitrate, minimum <= default <= maximum) are stored unchecked, as in release builds. Closes when: compared with the console.

## change webrtc::AudioNetworkAdaptorImpl::Config constructor and destructor

- [webrtc::AudioNetworkAdaptorImpl::Config constructor and destructor](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/AudioNetworkAdaptorConfig.cpp) (libwebrtc) - nothing is checked on the console. Adapted from [audio_network_adaptor_impl.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/modules/audio_coding/audio_network_adaptor/audio_network_adaptor_impl.cc), whose WebRTC notice and BSD licence head the file: the constructor validates writability via `GuestWritable_nid_no_patch` and writes null `event_log` through `GuestPtr::CopyOut`, and the defaulted destructor does nothing, as upstream, since the struct does not own the log. A null, inaccessible or non-writable config throws in the constructor. Closes when: compared with the console.

## change webrtc::AudioSourceInterface::options

- [webrtc::AudioSourceInterface::options](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/AudioSourceOptions.cpp) (libwebrtc) - nothing is checked on the console, and that the guest `std::optional` stores its flag after the value is assumed. Adapted from [media_stream_interface.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/api/media_stream_interface.cc), whose WebRTC notice and BSD licence head the file: the default returns `{}`, an `AudioOptions` with all eleven optionals of `audio_options.h` unset (`ios_force_software_aec_HACK` exists only under `WEBRTC_IOS`), written as 96 zero bytes via `GuestWritable_nid_no_patch` and `GuestBytes::CopyOut` through the hidden result pointer; `audio_network_adaptor_config` holds the 40-byte guest `std::string` at 40. The source is never read, as upstream's default does not read it; a null, inaccessible or non-writable result throws. Closes when: compared with the console.
