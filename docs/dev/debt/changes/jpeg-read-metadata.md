## add Callable exports

- [cT6Gbjt5xYE](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceJpegParser/ReadMetadata.cpp) (libSceJpegParser) - positive requested-key lists, GPS directories, MPF metadata and partially convertible Exif capture dates are unverified and throw. The firmware's temporary allocation sequence, duplicate-tag selection, date conversion under different console time zones and behavior with additional image frames have not been compared on a physical console. The default-key values and PlayStation MakerNote frames were measured by running the [13.42 module](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/issues/21873#issuecomment-6961554708) with stand-ins, including [ten MakerNote inputs](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/issues/21873#issuecomment-6961626975).

## add Callable exports

- [JPEG filename defaults](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceJpegParser/ReadMetadata.cpp) - the [JPEG 13.42 reading](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/issues/21873#issuecomment-6961554708) reports the extension follows GIF. The [GIF 13.42 path matrix](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/issues/21873#issuecomment-6961198994) uses the last dot in the whole path, even in a parent directory. JPEG reports a filename stem when ImageDescription is absent. Its bare and extensionless title cases have no separate firmware measurements; they follow the measured GIF rules here. The Exif payload is bounded by the 16-bit APP1 segment. A smaller firmware-specific text limit has not been measured.
