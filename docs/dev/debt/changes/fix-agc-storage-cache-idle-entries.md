# libSceAgcDriver

## change - [Storage-image cache entries]
- [Storage-image cache entries](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Graphics/src/ShaderResources.cpp) (libSceAgcDriver): past 2048 surfaces (`APS5_STORAGE_CACHE_ENTRIES`; `0` turns the limit off) the storage-image cache evicts its least recently used surface only after 10 seconds of cache activity without a use (`APS5_STORAGE_CACHE_IDLE_MS`), and at eight times the limit regardless of age; the byte budget evicts as before. Activity is host time between lookups, at most one second per gap, so a stall without lookups does not age every surface. The limit bounds small surfaces at new addresses that never reach the byte budget yet hold Vulkan objects and CPU memory (a local Planet of Lana build grew by about 50 a minute); PPSA38500 reuses about 10,700 small surfaces every few seconds, which a plain limit of 2048 rebuilt about 3,000 times a second. The 10 seconds, the one-second gap, the factor of eight and 2048 are choices, not measured.
