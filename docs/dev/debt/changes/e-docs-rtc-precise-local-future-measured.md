# libSceRtc
## change [sceRtcFormatRFC3339PreciseLocalTime]
- [sceRtcFormatRFC3339PreciseLocalTime](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceRtc/Export.cpp) (libSceRtc) - a tick more than 1439 minutes past 9999 returns `0x80B50008` before the host time zone is read, because no zone in range brings its local time back to 9999 or earlier. Derived from the measured local-result check above. A PS5 (firmware 11.60, payload process; a title process may differ) returned `0x80B50008` and wrote nothing for the tick of 9999-12-31T23:59:59.999999 UTC, that tick plus 1 microsecond, plus 1438, 1439 and 1440 minutes, plus 1440 minutes and 1 microsecond, and plus 60 days. The probe did not log the sign of the offset. That the last microsecond of 9999 UTC already returned `0x80B50008` is what a zone east of UTC gives (local time past 9999), so the zone of that console is probably east of UTC; this is an inference, and the 1439-minute boundary is not decided by these cases.
