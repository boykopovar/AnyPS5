# shader

## change [v_fma_f64 and v_div_fmas_f64 denormals]

- [v_fma_f64 and v_div_fmas_f64 denormals](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/Float64Instructions.cpp) follow the `FLOAT_MODE` f16/f64 denormal field, measured on RDNA3 (gfx1101, RX 7700 XT); the PS5's gfx1013 is not measured. `v_fma_f64` flushes denormal operands to a zero of their sign when bit 0 is clear (0 and 2); `v_div_fmas_f64` keeps them. When bit 1 is clear (0 and 1) both flush a result rounding to 53 significant bits (unbounded exponent) below 2^-1022 to a zero of its sign, `v_div_fmas_f64` after its VCC scale by 2^±128. On RDNA2 (gfx1036), IEEE=0/1, a product rounding up to 2^-1022 stays in all four (`0x2000000000000001 * 0x1ffffffffffffffe`) while the exact tie `0x3fefffffffffffff * 0x0010000000000000` flushes in 0 and 1; `v_div_fmas_f64` matches. gfx1035 matches in 0 and 3 (6,000 rows, 300 random biased triples); gfx1031 (RX 6750 XT, 32.0.21045.29003) in all four at IEEE=0/1 (8,913 triples; NaN payloads unchecked). Without a known float mode, lower as before.
