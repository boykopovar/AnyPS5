# libc

## change [numpunct<char> vtable]

- [numpunct<char> vtable](../../../core/libs/prx/libc/src/LocaleSupport.cpp) (libc, NID `lJnP-cn0cvQ`) is eleven `uintptr_t` slots matching `st_size` 88 on title `libc.prx` from PPSA26344 (offset-to-top, typeinfo, two destructors, `_Incref`/`_Decref`, and five `do_*` virtuals). Slots 2 through 10 hold libc's `numpunct<char>` destructors, the reference counting of the other facets, and `do_decimal_point`, `do_thousands_sep`, `do_grouping`, `do_falsename` and `do_truename`, in the declaration order of [microsoft/STL `xfacet`](https://github.com/microsoft/STL/blob/219514876ea86491de191ceaa88632616bbc0f19/stl/inc/xfacet#L26-L36) and [`xlocnum`](https://github.com/microsoft/STL/blob/219514876ea86491de191ceaa88632616bbc0f19/stl/inc/xlocnum#L160-L222). Offset-to-top and typeinfo remain zero, unlike the console's relocated entries. `_Getcat` still builds its facet on libc's internal table with static strings.

## add Functional

- [numpunct<char> _Init and _Tidy](../../../core/libs/prx/libc/src/LocaleSupport.cpp) (libc, NIDs `1LvbNeZZJ-o`, `fFnht9SPed8`) follow Dinkumware's `numpunct` ([crt.vc12 `xlocnum`](https://github.com/ganboing/crt.vc12/blob/f1d47126bf13d1c9dccaa6c53b72217fd0474968/src/xlocnum#L161-L221)): `_Init` copies `localeconv()`'s grouping, or `""` when `_Isdef` is set, and `"false"` and `"true"` (`xlocinfo` lines 125-128 and 165-173) into `operator new[]` blocks, frees them and rethrows if one fails, and takes `.` and `,` when `_Isdef` is set or the first byte of the decimal point and thousands separator otherwise; `_Tidy` releases the three blocks with `operator delete[]`. microsoft/STL allocates with `calloc` and frees with `free` instead (`xlocale` lines 563-579, `xlocnum` lines 225-229). A null facet throws `std::invalid_argument` where the console faults. The console's bodies were not read.
- [numpunct<char> constructors and destructors](../../../core/libs/prx/libc/src/LocaleSupport.cpp) (libc, NIDs `TEtyeXjcZ0w`, `1eVdDzPtzD4`, `CDm+TUClE7E`, `Cb1hI+w9nyU`, `WK24j1F3rCU`, `yIn4l8OO1zA`, `qEob3o53s2M`, `xFva4yxsVW8`, `Lf6h5krl2fA`) follow [crt.vc12 `xlocnum`](https://github.com/ganboing/crt.vc12/blob/f1d47126bf13d1c9dccaa6c53b72217fd0474968/src/xlocnum#L107-L145) and [microsoft/STL `xlocnum`](https://github.com/microsoft/STL/blob/219514876ea86491de191ceaa88632616bbc0f19/stl/inc/xlocnum#L137-L168): each stores the vtable and the low 32 bits of the reference count (`xlocale` lines 160-163), the `size_t` form runs `_Init` on a `"C"` `_Locinfo` and replaces a zero separator with `,`, and the destructors run `_Tidy`, the deleting one then `operator delete`. The named forms build `_Locinfo`, which here accepts only `"C"`. A null facet throws `std::invalid_argument`. The console's bodies were not read.
