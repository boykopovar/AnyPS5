# libSceGnmDriver

## change [sceGnmMapComputeQueue, sceGnmDingDong, sceGnmUnmapComputeQueue]

- [sceGnmMapComputeQueue, sceGnmDingDong, sceGnmUnmapComputeQueue](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceGnmDriver/Export.cpp) (libSceGnmDriver, PS4) - not measured on a console: the queue ids, results for refused inputs, and when the read offset moves. Arguments from CUSA00419's call sites 0x1a4df99, 0x1a4e0be, 0x1a4e16b and 0x1a4dfdc (sizes and offsets in dwords). A pipe above 6, a queue above 7, a ring not 256-byte aligned or not a power of two in size, and a read offset not 4-byte aligned throw; firmware 10.71's `sceGnmMapComputeQueueWithPriority` returns 0x80D17000-0x80D17004 for them (#16238), as [shadPS4](https://github.com/shadps4-emu/shadPS4/blob/4788f0b108/src/core/libraries/gnmdriver/gnmdriver.cpp) does. `sceGnmDingDong` sets the read offset when it returns, before the packets run; unmapping does not wait. A missing ring or read offset, a second map, an unknown id, a REWIND in a ring, and an offset at or past its end or in a packet throw. With `APS5_GNM_ORDER_COMPUTE_GRAPHICS`, each `sceGnmDingDong` waits for queued graphics submissions to settle (and every DingDong after another one also waits), and subsequent graphics submissions wait for compute work, keeping passes sharing GDS counters in submission order; serializes the queues and can wait up to 5 s while holding the ring or submission lock.
