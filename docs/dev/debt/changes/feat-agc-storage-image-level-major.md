# libSceAgcDriver

## change [PS4 (GCN) thick and layered surfaces]

- [PS4 (GCN) thick and layered surfaces](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Graphics/src/TextureTiling.cpp) (libSceAgcDriver, PS4) - a 3D texture in tile mode 19 (`Thick_1dThick`) uses addrlib's 8x8x4 `ADDR_THICK` micro tiles, depth padded to 4, each level as slabs of four slices; nonzero levels below four slices degrade to 1D thin with one slice per stride and pitch raised until the slice is 256-byte aligned (addrlib `ComputeSurfaceInfoMicroTiled` and SI `HwlGetSizeAdjustmentMicroTiled`); a block-compressed volume, a thick mode on a non-3D T# and a storage image of a volume with mips throw. Modes 15 and 20 to 26 throw, and so does a depth plane of a layered surface with several mip levels; a storage image of a layered surface with several mip levels (stored level by level) is tracked as a single unit without block or slice tracking. A sampled layered surface with several mip levels is stored level by level, and a 3D surface in a thin mode the same way, its level n holding max(1, depth >> n) slices, rounded up to a power of two with `POW2_PAD` as its width and height are, following [ac_surface](https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa/-/blob/mesa-24.0.0/src/amd/common/ac_surface.c#L776) and addrlib's [PostComputeMipLevel](https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa/-/blob/mesa-24.0.0/src/amd/addrlib/src/core/addrlib1.cpp#L3818-L3842). Neither is checked on a console; [GcnDetile.cpp](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/tests/execution/GcnDetile.cpp) covers the thin and depth modes; mode 19 has layout tests only.
