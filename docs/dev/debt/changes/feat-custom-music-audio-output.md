# libSceCustomMusicAudioOut

## add Functional

- [sceCustomMusicAudioOutOutput and sceCustomMusicAudioOutGetLastOutputTime](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceCustomMusicAudioOut/Export.cpp) (libSceCustomMusicAudioOut) - route stereo PCM and its submission time through AudioOut based on the [firmware 13.42 readings](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/issues/21885#issuecomment-6947728129) ([output and teardown](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/issues/21885#issuecomment-6951125575)). Guest memory transfers are checked, and the host frame count is limited to 32768 because SDL may round the 16-bit count up to a power of two. The extra import `lTvit2qjY0o` and lifecycle helpers remain unmodeled. The all-music level is process-local until the system-setting imports are identified. Closes when: the missing imports and helpers are understood and the output path is compared with a console run.

- [sceCustomMusicAudioOutSetVolume and sceCustomMusicAudioOutSetAllMusicVolume](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceCustomMusicAudioOut/Export.cpp) (libSceCustomMusicAudioOut) - retain the requested level while muted and feed silence to AudioOut until unmuted. The firmware reading shows direct level writes. The interaction between mute and volume changes is unmeasured on hardware. Closes when: the sequence of mute, volume change and PCM output is measured on a PS5.

- [sceCustomMusicAudioOutInitialize, sceCustomMusicAudioOutFinalize and sceCustomMusicAudioOutOutput](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceCustomMusicAudioOut/Export.cpp) (libSceCustomMusicAudioOut) - initialization forwards open errors unchanged as in the firmware reading. That reading ignores the initial gain result. On the host, a failed gain update closes the new port, returns the mapped error and keeps the previous port. Finalization retains the port on a close error, as in the firmware reading. Host buffer checks return the generic AudioOut failure for unreadable PCM. The wrapper holds its lock through AudioOut submission and drain to keep the port alive. Pointer faults and the timing of concurrent calls remain unmeasured on hardware.
