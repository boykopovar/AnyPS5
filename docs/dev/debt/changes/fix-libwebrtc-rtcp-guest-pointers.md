# libwebrtc

## change webrtc::rtcp::CommonHeader::Parse

- [webrtc::rtcp::CommonHeader::Parse](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/RtcpCommonHeader.cpp) (libwebrtc) - adapted from [common_header.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/modules/rtp_rtcp/source/rtcp_packet/common_header.cc), with its WebRTC copyright notice and BSD licence at the top of the file, on the 16-byte layout of `common_header.h`: packet type, count or format and padding size at 0 to 2, payload size at 4 and payload pointer at 8. Header structs and packet bytes are copied through `GuestPtr`/`GuestBytes` and `header` is probed with `GuestWritable_nid_no_patch` before writing. Its checks run in the same order, so a short buffer or a wrong version leaves the header untouched and the later failures leave the fields already parsed. The `RTC_LOG(LS_WARNING)` message each failure logs upstream is not emitted. A null or non-writable header, a null or inaccessible buffer of 4 bytes or more, or an inaccessible padding byte throws. Closes when: the warnings are logged through libwebrtc's logging and the parse is compared with the console.

## change webrtc::rtcp::App

- [webrtc::rtcp::App](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/RtcpApp.cpp) (libwebrtc) - layout, vtable and allocator are unverified on the console. Layout: vtable, `sender_ssrc_` at 8, `sub_type_` at 12, `name_` at 16, `rtc::Buffer` at 24. A local vtable holds D1, D0, `BlockLength` and `Create` (callback as two pointers). Packet objects, common headers, indices, payloads and destination buffers go through `GuestPtr`/`GuestBytes` and `GuestWritable_nid_no_patch` probes before mutating guest memory. The buffer grows through guest `new[]` to max(size, 1.5 x capacity). No `RTC_LOG`, and `Parse` does not check the packet type; null, inaccessible or non-writable arguments, or a full buffer without a callback, throw. Adapted from [app.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/modules/rtp_rtcp/source/rtcp_packet/app.cc), [rtcp_packet.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/modules/rtp_rtcp/source/rtcp_packet.cc) and `BufferT::SetData` in [buffer.h](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/rtc_base/buffer.h), the same at M127. Closes when: compared with the console.

## change webrtc::rtcp::Nack::Parse

- [webrtc::rtcp::Nack::Parse](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/RtcpNack.cpp) (libwebrtc) - console ABI and behaviour are unverified. Adapted from [nack.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/modules/rtp_rtcp/source/rtcp_packet/nack.cc) and `ParseCommonFeedback` in [rtpfb.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/modules/rtp_rtcp/source/rtcp_packet/rtpfb.cc), retaining their notices and BSD licence. M127 parsing is identical. GCC 15 probing the upstream headers with dependency shims gives 64 bytes: SSRCs at 8 and 12, packed and ID vectors at 16 and 40. Three-pointer vectors use guest new/delete and bulk `GuestBytes::CopyOut` after host validation; structs, indices, headers and payloads use `GuestPtr`/`GuestBytes` and `GuestWritable_nid_no_patch`. Short payloads preserve state; trailing bytes are ignored, order and duplicates retained, and BLP expansion wraps. Release DCHECKs and warning logs are omitted. Null, inaccessible or non-writable arguments, inconsistent vector triples, or an inaccessible payload throw. Closes when: console ABI and behaviour are compared.

## change webrtc::rtcp::TransportFeedback constructor, destructor and Parse

- [webrtc::rtcp::TransportFeedback constructor, destructor and Parse](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/RtcpTransportFeedback.cpp) (libwebrtc) - adapted from M134 [transport_feedback.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/modules/rtp_rtcp/source/rtcp_packet/transport_feedback.cc), retaining its notice and BSD licence. M127 agrees on lifecycle, parser and chunk decoding. GCC compilation of the real class headers gives 152 bytes: timestamp at 32, three vector triples at 40/64/88, LastChunk at 112 and size at 144; dependencies use an int64 timestamp and vectors with pointer triples. Structs, common headers, payloads and vector slices use `GuestPtr`/`GuestBytes` and `GuestWritable_nid_no_patch`; guest allocations use guest new/delete. The destructor pair occupies vtable slots 0/1; BlockLength and Create throw; RTTI is absent. Parse preserves release failure state, unsigned small and signed large deltas, absent timestamps and the sticky timestamp flag. Null, inaccessible or non-writable arguments, or inconsistent vector triples throw. Console revision, layout and vtable remain unverified. Closes when: compared with the console.
