# shader

## change [T# MIN_LOD without VK_EXT_image_view_min_lod]

- [T# MIN_LOD without VK_EXT_image_view_min_lod](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Optimization/src/DescriptorBindingBuilder.cpp) - without the extension (MoltenVK), direct images take the clamp from a specialization constant. That raises λ where the view clamp raises only the level, so it throws where they differ: a paired S# with different magnification and minification filters (at λ 0 AMD RDNA 4's view clamp minified, spec and NVIDIA magnify, gfx1013 unmeasured), or with mips and a lower MAX_LOD or a bias on an explicit LOD; unnormalized or run-time S#s; `image_get_lod`; gathers at a clamp of a level or more; emulated depth-compare and 11:11:10. An S# holding the LOD at 0 gets MIN_LOD = MAX_LOD = the clamp unless it also samples a table or a mipmapped image of another clamp. An S# bias on an implicit or gradient LOD is unchecked (llvmpipe samples one level lower at a clamp of 2 and a bias of -1). Image tables throw; fetches below the clamp stay undefined.

## add Functional

- [T# MIN_LOD on a device with the extension](../../../core/shader/recompiler/SpirvBackend/src/SpirvImageEmitter.cpp) - the startup probe moves a device whose view clamp changes `image_sample_d_cl` (Mesa ANV) to the shader lane above, where fragment registration modules read the clamp from the T# words instead of a specialization constant. Only direct images take that lane: an image table entry keeps the view clamp, since the extension stays enabled, so on ANV an `image_sample_d_cl` of a table entry with a clamp still loses its gradient LOD and a size or level query of it reads 0. The shader lane adds a `MinLod` operand to a derivative sample only where the clamp is not 0 (a branch on the specialization constant, or on the T# words in registration modules), because ANV lowers `Grad` with `MinLod` to an explicit-LOD sample even at 0. ANV also adds an S# bias to a gradient LOD after the clamp operand, in both lanes, and nothing corrects it. Probe-switched devices commit every mip.
