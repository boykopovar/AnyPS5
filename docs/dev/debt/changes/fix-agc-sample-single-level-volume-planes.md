# shader

## change [2D biased sampling through a one-slice, single-level 3D T#]

- [2D sampling through a one-slice, single-level 3D T#](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Optimization/src/ResourceMaterializer.cpp) supplies a zero third coordinate and keeps the sample's own operation (implicit LOD, LOD zero, explicit LOD or bias), with normalized point minification and magnification and no linear mip filtering. Measured on RDNA2 (AMD integrated gfx1036, driver 32.0.21036.18, Windows 11) by running an OpenCL-loaded native kernel with `image_sample_b` DIM 2D against 16x16x1 R32_UINT and RGBA8_UNORM 3D images: biases -16, -0.5, 0, 0.5 and 16 read the (u,v) texel in slice zero, including wrapped coordinates, point min/max reduction and the title's sampler with Z border addressing. Wave32, IEEE off, DX10 clamp on, denormals preserved, round to nearest even, FP16 overflow off; on a second gfx1036 rig (driver 31.0.22044.1) wave64 matches wave32 for 8_8_8_8_UNORM, 8_8_8_8_UINT and 32_UINT, wrap and clamp addressing, and biases -16 to 16; point min/max reduction, Z border addressing and the PS5's gfx1013 remain unmeasured at wave64. With `tools/hw-oracle` on an RX 6650 XT (gfx1032, Linux, hsa-rocr 10.1, wave32, IEEE off, DX10 clamp on, denormals preserved, round to nearest even, FP16 overflow off), `image_sample`, `image_sample_lz`, `image_sample_l`, `image_sample_b`, `image_sample_d` and `image_load` DIM 2D through a 64x64x1 8_8_8_8_UNORM 3D T# return the same values as through the equivalent 2D T# in all 32 rows each, at texel centres and 0.3 texels off them with bilinear XY and linear Z filtering and clamp-to-last-texel addressing. Pixel-stage, direct, non-depth, unpacked 32-bit samples without derivative adjustment, offsets or gradients other than those four forms are accepted; extra slices, any nonzero mip range, linear or anisotropic filtering, unnormalized coordinates, and FORCE_DEGAMMA with any form other than bias throw. Vulkan readback is checked on NVIDIA RTX 5070 and AMD integrated graphics for positive and negative bias and point weighted-average/min/max reduction, and on an RX 6650 XT (RADV) for LOD zero and implicit LOD.
