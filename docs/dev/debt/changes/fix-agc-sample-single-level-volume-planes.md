# shader

## change [2D biased sampling through a one-slice, single-level 3D T#]

- [2D sampling through a one-slice, single-level 3D T#](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Optimization/src/ResourceMaterializer.cpp) throws for extra slices, a nonzero mip range, linear or anisotropic filtering, unnormalized coordinates, gradients, offsets, comparison and derivative adjustment, and FORCE_DEGAMMA outside the bias form; gfx1013 is unmeasured. Pixel-stage, direct, unpacked 32-bit samples with implicit LOD, LOD zero, explicit LOD or bias supply a zero third coordinate under a normalized point sampler. On RDNA2, `image_sample_b` through 16x16x1 3D images reads the (u,v) texel of slice zero (gfx1036, OpenCL, wave32 and wave64), and `image_sample`, `_lz`, `_l`, `_b`, `_d` and `image_load` DIM 2D through a 64x64x1 3D T# match the equivalent 2D T# in every row, at and off texel centres with linear filtering (RX 6650 XT, `tools/hw-oracle`, wave32). Vulkan readback is checked on NVIDIA RTX 5070, AMD integrated graphics and RX 6650 XT (RADV).
