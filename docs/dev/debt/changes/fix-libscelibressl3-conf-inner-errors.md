# libSceLibreSsl3

## add Functional

- [X509V3_EXT_conf_nid inner errors](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceLibreSsl3/src/X509V3ExtConfNid.cpp) (libSceLibreSsl3) - an object identifier that does not parse, in `ASN1:OBJECT` or an extendedKeyUsage list, queues `ASN1_R_ILLEGAL_OBJECT` or `X509V3_R_INVALID_OBJECT_IDENTIFIER` without the parser's own reason before it: [LibreSSL 3.4.1](https://github.com/libressl/openbsd/blob/078b3d1df8ca2011c1605cd40418c58fa0f5b143/src/lib/libcrypto/asn1/a_object.c#L92-L213) and [4.0.0](https://github.com/libressl/openbsd/blob/ce488b78e0bd994780569df718596b9776ff681a/src/lib/libcrypto/asn1/a_object.c#L192-L294) give different reasons, such as `FIRST_NUM_TOO_LARGE` and `INVALID_SEPARATOR` for `bogus`. ASN1 reasons are queued without file, line or data. Not checked on a console. Closes when: compared with the console.
- [X509V3_EXT_conf_nid lone minus sign](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceLibreSsl3/src/X509V3ExtConfNid.cpp) (libSceLibreSsl3) - an `ASN1:INTEGER` or `ENUMERATED` value whose digits are a lone second minus sign, `--`, `0x-` or `-0x-`, throws: LibreSSL 3.4.1 then builds a negative zero that [`i2c_ASN1_INTEGER`](https://github.com/libressl/openbsd/blob/078b3d1df8ca2011c1605cd40418c58fa0f5b143/src/lib/libcrypto/asn1/a_int.c#L176-L192) encodes out of bounds, and [4.0.0](https://github.com/libressl/openbsd/blob/ce488b78e0bd994780569df718596b9776ff681a/src/lib/libcrypto/bn/bn_convert.c#L387-L488) encodes 0. Not checked on a console. Closes when: compared with the console.
- [X509V3_EXT_conf_nid BITLIST negative numbers](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceLibreSsl3/src/X509V3ExtConfNid.cpp) (libSceLibreSsl3) - a `BITLIST` number from `-9223372036854775809` down to `-18446744073709551615` fails here with `ASN1_R_LIST_ERROR`. LibreSSL 3.4.1's [`bitstr_cb`](https://github.com/libressl/openbsd/blob/078b3d1df8ca2011c1605cd40418c58fa0f5b143/src/lib/libcrypto/asn1/asn1_gen.c#L781-L801) wraps it with `strtoul` and [`ASN1_BIT_STRING_set_bit`](https://github.com/libressl/openbsd/blob/078b3d1df8ca2011c1605cd40418c58fa0f5b143/src/lib/libcrypto/asn1/a_bitstr.c#L194-L226) truncates it to an `int`: `-18446744073709551615` gives `03 02 06 40`, `-9223372036854775809` (`int` -1) `03 02 00 01`, and `-18446744071562067968` (`int` -2^31) fails with two `ERR_R_MALLOC_FAILURE` before `LIST_ERROR`; by the source, an `int` from -15 to -8 writes out of bounds. Measured on 3.4.1, not on 4.0.0. Not checked on a console. Closes when: these numbers are converted as LibreSSL does.
- [X509V3_EXT_conf_nid BITLIST numbers above INT_MAX](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceLibreSsl3/src/X509V3ExtConfNid.cpp) (libSceLibreSsl3) - a `BITLIST` number from `2147483648` to `9223372036854775807` throws. LibreSSL 3.4.1 truncates it to an `int`: `4294967296` gives bit 0 (`03 02 07 80`), and `2147483648` fails with two `ERR_R_MALLOC_FAILURE` before `LIST_ERROR`. By the source, an `int` from -15 to -8 writes out of bounds. Measured on 3.4.1, not on 4.0.0. Not checked on a console. Closes when: these numbers are converted as LibreSSL does.
