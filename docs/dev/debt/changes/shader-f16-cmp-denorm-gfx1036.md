# shader

## change [f16 compares]
- [f16 compares](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/CompareInstructions.cpp) (`v_cmp_X_f16` and `v_cmpx_X_f16` with X = eq, ge, gt, le, lg, lt, neq, nge, ngt, nle, nlg, nlt) flush their f16 denormal inputs to a zero of the same sign by bit 0 of the `FLOAT_MODE` f16 denormal field: modes 0 and 2 flush them, 1 and 3 keep them. Measured on RDNA2 (gfx1031, Radeon RX 6700 XT, AMD Windows driver 32.0.21045.5002 through HIP) over 256 operand pairs of zeros, denormals, minimum normals, ones, the largest finite value, infinities and quiet and signaling NaNs of both signs, in VOPC form, at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1 with each f16 denormal mode 0 to 3, f16 round to nearest even, DX10_CLAMP=1, f32 denormals kept and FP16_OVFL=0. Shaders without a known float mode keep the previous lowering. The PS5's gfx1013 is not measured. Corroborated on gfx1036 (Windows driver 31.0.22044.1, HIP) with `v_cmp_eq_f16` and `v_cmp_lt_f16` (VOP3) over 4 pairs at IEEE=0 and 1, denorm16 0..3.
