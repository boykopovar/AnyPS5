# libSceNKWebKit

## add Functional

- [Date strings](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceNKWebKit/src/JscDate.cpp) (libSceNKWebKit) - not checked on a console. `Date.parse`, `new Date(string)` and `JSObjectMakeDate` parse as [`DateCache::parseDate`](https://github.com/neonmodder123/PS5-WebKit-11.x/blob/80f77e94c63ee7202eec3d4b5b1a021dd89011ba/Source/JavaScriptCore/runtime/JSDateMath.cpp): WTF's ES5 parser, then its legacy one, both ported here. Local strings are converted by the engine's `Date` from their fields, where JavaScriptCore subtracts `localTimeOffset`. On Linux the engine uses the offset at the wall time read as UTC: an hour off for as many hours as the zone's UTC offset after a daylight saving change west of UTC, or before one east of it. On Windows it applies the host's current offset to every date, ignoring `TZ`. Replacing U+202F by a space follows the pinned source, possibly newer than the console module. What `Date` prints (`toString`, `toLocaleString`) is still the engine's. Closes when: compared with a console.
