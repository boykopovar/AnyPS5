# shader

## add Functional

- [v_rcp_f64, v_rsq_f64 and v_sqrt_f64 denormals](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/Float64Instructions.cpp) follow the `FLOAT_MODE` f16/f64 denormal field: a denormal input is read as a zero of its sign when bit 0 is clear (0 and 2), and a denormal result becomes a zero of its sign when bit 1 is clear (0 and 1), at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1. Measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036, AMD Windows driver 32.0.21042.62) with [tools/hw-oracle](../HW_ORACLE.md); the PS5's gfx1013 is not measured. The hardware results of all three are approximations, often only about 30 bits exact (`v_sqrt_f64(1.0)` is `0x3ff0000004000000`), where the translation returns the driver's correctly rounded value; only the exact rows are pinned by [Float64TranscendentalDenormals.cpp](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/tests/execution/Float64TranscendentalDenormals.cpp).

# libSceAgcDriver

## change do not model these denormal controls.

- The [shader float mode](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Execution/src/Driver/Shaders/ShaderRegistry.cpp) (`FLOAT_MODE`, `DX10_CLAMP`, `IEEE_MODE` and `FP16_OVFL` of `COMPUTE_PGM_RSRC1` and `SPI_SHADER_PGM_RSRC1_PS/GS/HS`, bit positions from Mesa's `gfx10.json` and `gfx103.json`) is decoded into `GuestContext::floatMode` and is part of the shader cache keys and the serialized request (#981). `IEEE_MODE` is read by `v_min_f32`, `v_max_f32`, `v_min3_f32`, `v_max3_f32` and `v_med3_f32`, their f16 forms, `v_min_f64` and `v_max_f64`, `IEEE_MODE` and the `FLOAT_MODE` output denormal bits by the output modifier, `DX10_CLAMP` by the clamp modifier, `FP16_OVFL` by every instruction with an f16 result (see its entry), and the whole mode by the 16-bit interpolation instructions (see their entries). With IEEE=1 a signaling NaN operand returns that NaN quieted (the first operand checked first, min3/max3 as two nested pairs, med3 as min3 when any operand is NaN), measured on RDNA2 (gfx1035) over every combination of ±0, ±1, ±inf, denormals, quiet and signaling NaNs with f32 denormals kept and flushed. IEEE=0 and unknown modes keep the previous f32 lowering, which matches IEEE=0. The f16 forms choose their NaN results on the bits: a NaN operand returns the other operand unchanged (src0 when both are NaN), and with IEEE=1 a signaling NaN operand returns that NaN quieted, in the same order as f32. `v_min_f64` and `v_max_f64` quiet a signaling NaN operand the same way with IEEE=1; with IEEE=0 they return src0 unchanged when src1 is NaN, otherwise src1 when src0 is NaN. Both were measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036) at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1 with f32 denormals kept and flushed and f16/f64 denormals kept; RDNA2 has no f64 min3, max3 or med3. Unknown modes follow IEEE=0. `v_min_f16` and `v_max_f16` flush each denormal input to its signed zero when bit 0 of the f16/f64 denormal field is clear (0 and 2); fields 1 and 3 preserve inputs, even when output flushing is enabled. Measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036) at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1, including signed zeros, NaNs and neg/abs modifiers. Unknown modes keep f16 denormals. `v_min_f64` and `v_max_f64` flush subnormal inputs to signed zero when bit 0 of the f16/f64 denormal field is clear (0 and 2); fields 1 and 3, and an unknown mode, preserve inputs. The fully flushed and preserved f64 behavior was [measured on gfx1036](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/2036); split-mode selection follows [LLVM's denormal-mode enumeration](https://llvm.org/docs/AMDGPUUsage.html#floating-point-denorm-mode-enumeration-values). `v_fma_f64`, `v_div_fmas_f64`, `v_div_scale_f64`, `v_ldexp_f64` and `v_trig_preop_f64` do not model these denormal controls; `v_rcp_f64`, `v_rsq_f64` and `v_sqrt_f64` do (see the shader debt). NaN quieting in f16 arithmetic (the unary instructions and the f32 arithmetic follow it, see below) still follows a single mode, as the entries below record. The mode comes from the registered shader header's `sh_registers`, the same source at registration and at draw/dispatch so the prepared artifact keys match. A registered shader keeps that mode when a title rewrites RSRC1; a raw compute program takes the live RSRC1.
