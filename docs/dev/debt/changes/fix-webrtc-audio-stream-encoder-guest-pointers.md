# libwebrtc

## change [webrtc::AudioEncoder::Encode]

- [webrtc::AudioEncoder::Encode](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/AudioEncoderEncode.cpp) (libwebrtc) - nothing is checked on the console, and the `TRACE_EVENT0` is not emitted. Adapted from [audio_encoder.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/api/audio_codecs/audio_encoder.cc), whose WebRTC notice and BSD licence head the file; `Encode` and the virtual order of `audio_encoder.h` are the same at M127 and M134. It calls `NumChannels` and `SampleRateHz` (slots 3 and 2), then `EncodeImpl` (slot 29) with the caller's result storage, and returns it. Where upstream's `RTC_CHECK_EQ`s stop the process, it throws: when the audio is not `NumChannels() * SampleRateHz() / 100` samples, before encoding, and when the bytes `EncodeImpl` appends to the `rtc::Buffer` differ from the `encoded_bytes` at offset 0 of the result. A null result, encoder, vtable, virtual function or output buffer throws; the vtable, the buffer size and `encoded_bytes` are read through `GuestPtr`, and an unreadable encoder, vtable slot, output buffer or result throws `std::invalid_argument`. Closes when: compared with the console.

## change [webrtc::AudioDecoder::ParseResult move constructor and destructor]

- [webrtc::AudioDecoder::ParseResult move constructor and destructor](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/AudioDecoder.cpp) (libwebrtc) - nothing is checked on the console. Both are defaulted in [audio_decoder.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/api/audio_codecs/audio_decoder.cc), on the 16-byte layout of `audio_decoder.h`, the same at M127 and M134: `timestamp` at 0, `priority` at 4 and the frame pointer at 8. The move copies the timestamp and priority and takes the frame, leaving the source's null. The destructor deletes a frame through its virtual deleting destructor, slot 1 because the complete destructor takes slot 0, which is not checked on the console; a frame without a vtable throws, and a result without a frame does nothing. The objects and the frame's vtable are read through `GuestPtr` and written after `GuestWritable_nid_no_patch` checks both objects; a null or unreadable result or source, an unwritable one in the move, or an unreadable frame vtable throws `std::invalid_argument` without changing either object. Closes when: compared with the console.
