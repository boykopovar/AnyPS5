# shader

## change [v_add_f64 and v_mul_f64 denormals]
- [v_add_f64 and v_mul_f64 denormals](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/Float64Instructions.cpp) follow the `FLOAT_MODE` f16/f64 denormal field; the PS5's gfx1013 is not measured. Inputs flush to a zero of their sign when bit 0 is clear (0 and 2). When bit 1 is clear (0 and 1), a sum below 2^-1022, or a product whose exact value rounded to 53 significant bits with an unbounded exponent is below 2^-1022, flushes to a zero of its sign, so `0x2000000000000001 * 0x1ffffffffffffffe` (exactly 2^-1022 - 2^-1126) rounds to 2^-1022 and is kept. Measured on RDNA2 (gfx1031, RX 6700 XT, AMD Windows driver 32.0.21045.5002, HIP) at IEEE=0 and 1, denorm16 0..3, over 576 pairs of 24 f64 values (zeros, denormals, minimum normals, values next to 1 and to 2^-1022, products just below 2^-1022, the largest finite value, infinities), with neg, abs and clamp. Shaders without a known float mode keep the previous lowering. Corroborated on gfx1036 over 3 pairs at denorm16 0..3.
