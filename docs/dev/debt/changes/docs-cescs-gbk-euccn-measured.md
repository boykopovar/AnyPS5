# libSceCesCs
## change [GBK (CP936) and EUC-CN conversions]
- [GBK (CP936) and EUC-CN conversions](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceCesCs/Export.cpp) (libSceCesCs) - `sceCesUcsProfileInitGbkCp936` and `sceCesUcsProfileInitEucCnGb2312` mark the caller's sheet with an 8-byte tag; a PS5 (firmware 11.60, payload process; a title process may differ) left the last non-zero byte of a zero-filled 2048-byte sheet at offset 84 for both, so its layout is larger and not known. Both use one table ([Cp936.hpp](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceCesCs/Cp936.hpp)) read from Microsoft's [CP936.TXT](https://unicode.org/Public/MAPPINGS/VENDORS/MICSFT/WINDOWS/CP936.TXT); the EUC-CN profile accepts only its `0xA1`-`0xFE` lead and trail bytes and gives the CP936 forms of `0xA1A4` (U+00B7) and `0xA1AA` (U+2014), as the console does. The console's GBK table matched it in all 126 leads (`sceCesMbcToUtf32` over every trail byte: a CRC32 of the results and their count of successes) and in all 16 reverse blocks of 4096 code points over U+0000-U+FFFF. Its EUC-CN profile gave no result for the trails `0xA1`-`0xB0`, `0xE3`, `0xE4`, `0xEF`, `0xF0`, `0xFD` and `0xFE` of lead `0xA2` (72 of 94 convert), `0xB9`-`0xC0` and `0xD9`-`0xFE` of `0xA6` (48) and `0xBB`-`0xC4` and `0xEA`-`0xFE` of `0xA8` (63), where this converts 82, 67 and 67 cells; 91 of the 94 leads and 15 of the 16 reverse blocks matched, and in the block U+0000-U+0FFF it converted 311 code points where this converts 282, among them U+0080-U+008D and U+0090-U+009F, which this does not. Errors, their precedence and the length pointers follow the Shift-JIS forms above and are unverified.
