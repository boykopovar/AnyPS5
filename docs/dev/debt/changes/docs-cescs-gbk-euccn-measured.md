# libSceCesCs
## change [GBK (CP936) and EUC-CN conversions]
- [GBK (CP936) and EUC-CN conversions](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceCesCs/Export.cpp) (libSceCesCs) - `sceCesUcsProfileInitGbkCp936` and `sceCesUcsProfileInitEucCnGb2312` mark the caller's sheet with an 8-byte tag; a PS5 (firmware 11.60, payload process; a title process may differ) left the last non-zero byte of a zero-filled 2048-byte sheet at offset 84 for both, so its layout is larger and not known. Both use one table ([Cp936.hpp](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceCesCs/Cp936.hpp)) read from Microsoft's [CP936.TXT](https://unicode.org/Public/MAPPINGS/VENDORS/MICSFT/WINDOWS/CP936.TXT), and the console's GBK table matched it in all 126 leads (`sceCesMbcToUtf32` over every trail byte: a CRC32 of the results and their count of successes) and in all 16 reverse blocks of 4096 code points over U+0000-U+FFFF. The EUC-CN profile takes the `0xA1`-`0xFE` lead and trail bytes of that table except the cells the console's EUC-CN profile does not convert: the trails `0xA1`-`0xB0`, `0xE3`, `0xE4`, `0xEF`, `0xF0`, `0xFD` and `0xFE` of lead `0xA2` (72 of 94 convert), `0xB9`-`0xC0` and `0xD9`-`0xFE` of `0xA6` (48) and `0xBB`-`0xC4` and `0xEA`-`0xFE` of `0xA8` (63); the cells sampled there answered `0x805C0020` with length 2. It gives the CP936 forms of `0xA1A4` (U+00B7) and `0xA1AA` (U+2014), reads the 30 bytes `0x80`-`0x8D` and `0x90`-`0x9F` as single characters of the same value and writes those scalars as one byte, and refuses the byte `0x0E` (`0x805C0014`, length 1) and U+000E (`0x805C0020`, length 0), as the console does (the other 127 ASCII values convert both ways). The console still encodes the 33 scalars of the cells it leaves out to their CP936 bytes, as this does, so the round trip fails for them. Errors, their precedence and the length pointers follow the Shift-JIS forms above and are unverified.
