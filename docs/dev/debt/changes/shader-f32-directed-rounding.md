# shader

## change [v_fma_f32, v_fmac_f32, v_fmamk_f32 and v_fmaak_f32 in denormal modes 1 and 2]

- [v_fma_f32, v_fmac_f32, v_fmamk_f32 and v_fmaak_f32 in denormal modes 1 and 2](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/VectorInstructions.cpp) throw with a directed f32 round mode too, and when the module cannot declare `DenormPreserve 32`; the PS5's gfx1013 is not measured. Mode 2 flushes denormal inputs to zeros of their sign and rounds the fused result once with gradual underflow, the host's `Fma` under `DenormPreserve 32`. Mode 1 keeps the inputs and flushes a result that is tiny after rounding to 24 bits with an unbounded exponent, even with a nonzero addend: a fused result the host's `Fma` rounds into the denormal range is flushed to a zero of its sign, and a zero addend takes the product check of the f32 denormals entry. An exact sum in the half-ulp band the host rounds up to 2^-126 is kept, not flushed (console `0x807fffff * 0x3f7fffff + 0x80000001` gives `0x80000000`, here `0x80800000`); the exact sum is not computed. Measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036, AMD Windows driver 32.0.21030.31 through HIP) over 5,596 triples (16^3 of 16 boundary values, and 1,500 random ones with products near 2^-126) at IEEE 0 and 1 in every f32 denormal mode at nearest even. [F32FmaDenormalMode1.cpp](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/tests/execution/F32FmaDenormalMode1.cpp) checks both IEEE modes; [F32FmaDenormalMode2.cpp](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/tests/execution/F32FmaDenormalMode2.cpp) checks mode 2.

## change [v_add_f32, v_sub_f32, v_subrev_f32, v_mul_f32 and v_mul_legacy_f32 in denormal modes 1 and 2]

- [v_add_f32, v_sub_f32, v_subrev_f32, v_mul_f32 and v_mul_legacy_f32 in denormal modes 1 and 2](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/VectorInstructions.cpp) throw with a directed f32 round mode too (`v_mul_legacy_f32` under every directed mode, which it does not model), and when the module cannot declare `DenormPreserve 32` (no `shaderDenormPreserveFloat32`, or the hardware f32 flush in use); the PS5's gfx1013 is not measured. Otherwise mode 1 keeps denormal inputs and flushes a result below 2^-126 to a zero of its sign (a product only when it stays below 2^-126 after rounding to 24 bits with an unbounded exponent), and mode 2 flushes denormal inputs and keeps a denormal result. Measured on RDNA2 (gfx1030, RX 6800) over 1,296 operand pairs, 896 products around 2^-126 and 1,632 with a constant factor, at IEEE 0 and 1 in every f32 denormal mode, with DX10_CLAMP=1, f16 denormals kept and FP16_OVFL=0.

## add Functional

- [Scalar f32 arithmetic in a directed round mode](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/FloatInstructions.cpp) (`v_add_f32`, `v_sub_f32`, `v_subrev_f32`, `v_mul_f32`, `v_mac_f32`, `v_fma_f32`, `v_madmk_f32`, `v_madak_f32` and `v_mad_f32`) follows the `FLOAT_MODE` f32 round field: the exact product-sum is computed in double precision (or integer arithmetic without Float64) and rounded once toward the declared direction; the separately rounded forms round the product and then the sum, each toward that direction. `v_fma_f32` with op_sel half sources keeps the fused nearest-even lowering with the tiny-product flush rather than the declared direction (unverified). `v_mul_legacy_f32`, `v_mullit_f32`, `v_fract_f32`, `v_div_scale_f32` and `v_div_fixup_f32` still throw in a directed f32 round mode. The PS5's gfx1013 is not measured.
