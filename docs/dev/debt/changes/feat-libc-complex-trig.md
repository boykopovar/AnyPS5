# libc

## add Functional

- [clog, csqrt and cpow](../../../core/libs/prx/libc/src/FloatBuiltins.cpp) (libc) - the `double` forms compute through `std::complex<double>` as C99 Annex G defines them, including the branch cuts. The `float` forms are not exported yet. The console's results at the branch cuts and for special values are unverified.
- [ctanh and ctan](../../../core/libs/prx/libc/src/ComplexTanh.cpp) (libc) - FreeBSD 11.4's [`s_ctanh.c`](https://github.com/freebsd/freebsd-src/blob/8c639945a44588f6cfdddfb057bb5aad88735da0/lib/msun/src/s_ctanh.c) over this library's msun `tan`, `sin`, `cos`, `sinh` and `exp`, sharing the code of `ctanhl` and switching to `±1` from a real part of magnitude 22, as FreeBSD does. The console's results are unverified.
- [cacos, cacosh, casin, casinh, catanh and catan](../../../core/libs/prx/libc/src/ComplexInverseTrig.cpp) (libc) - FreeBSD 11.4's [`catrig.c`](https://github.com/freebsd/freebsd-src/blob/8c639945a44588f6cfdddfb057bb5aad88735da0/lib/msun/src/catrig.c), sharing the code of the `long double` forms. The console's results are unverified.
- [ccos, ccosh, cexp, csin, csinh, ccosl, ccoshl, cexpl, csinl and csinhl](../../../core/libs/prx/libc/src/Math.cpp) (libc) - the console's results are unverified. They follow FreeBSD 11.4's [`s_ccosh.c`](https://github.com/freebsd/freebsd-src/blob/8c639945a44588f6cfdddfb057bb5aad88735da0/lib/msun/src/s_ccosh.c), [`s_csinh.c`](https://github.com/freebsd/freebsd-src/blob/8c639945a44588f6cfdddfb057bb5aad88735da0/lib/msun/src/s_csinh.c) and [`s_cexp.c`](https://github.com/freebsd/freebsd-src/blob/8c639945a44588f6cfdddfb057bb5aad88735da0/lib/msun/src/s_cexp.c) with `k_exp.c`, over this library's msun functions. FreeBSD 11.4 has no `long double` forms: `cexpl` is the later [`ld80/s_cexpl.c`](https://github.com/freebsd/freebsd-src/blob/0dd5a5603e7a33d976f8e6015620bbc79839c609/lib/msun/ld80/s_cexpl.c), and `ccoshl` and `csinhl` take its overflow thresholds and switch to `exp(|x|) / 2` from 64, as `e_coshl.c` does, instead of 22.
