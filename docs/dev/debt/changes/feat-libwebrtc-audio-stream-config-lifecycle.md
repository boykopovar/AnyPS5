# libwebrtc

## add Public source implementations

- [webrtc::AudioSendStream::Config::SendCodecSpec constructor and destructor](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/AudioStreamConfig.cpp) (libwebrtc) - nothing is checked on the console. Follows [audio_send_stream.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/call/audio_send_stream.cc), whose WebRTC notice and BSD licence head the file, on the 120-byte layout of `audio_send_stream.h`: `payload_type` at 0, the format at 8, flags at 88 and three `optional<int>` at 92, 100 and 108. M127 adds `transport_cc_enabled` at 89 and moves `enable_non_sender_rtt` to 90; the constructor clears bytes 88 to 91, so both revisions get the same bytes. The format is copied and destroyed through the `SdpAudioFormat` exports. A null or unwritable spec or an invalid format throws. Closes when: compared with the console.
- [webrtc::AudioSendStream::Stats and Config destructors](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/AudioStreamConfig.cpp) (libwebrtc) - nothing is checked on the console. Both are defaulted in [audio_send_stream.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/call/audio_send_stream.cc), the same at M127 but for `absl::optional`. The 360-byte `Stats` frees `codec_name` at 64 and the `report_block_datas` buffer at 328, whose elements are trivial. The 472-byte `Config` releases, in reverse member order, the references at 464, 456 and 384 through `Release`, slot 1 in [ref_count.h](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/api/ref_count.h), `track_id` at 408, an engaged `send_codec_spec` at 256 and `audio_network_adaptor_config` at 208, then `c_name`, the `RtpExtension` vector, `mid` and `rid` of `Rtp`. Malformed members throw before anything is released. Closes when: compared with the console.
- [webrtc::AudioReceiveStreamInterface::Stats and Config destructors](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/AudioStreamConfig.cpp) (libwebrtc) - nothing is checked on the console. Both are defaulted in [audio_receive_stream.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/call/audio_receive_stream.cc), the same at M127 but for `absl::optional` and `Stats` fields after `codec_name`, the only member `Stats` frees, at 72. The 160-byte `Config` releases the references at 152, 144 and 112 through `Release` slot 1, destroys each node of the `decoder_map` tree at 88 (120-byte nodes, format at 40) through the `SdpAudioFormat` destructor and guest `operator delete`, then frees `sync_group` at 48. Malformed strings, trees, formats or references throw before anything is released. Closes when: compared with the console.
