# libkernel

## change [sceKernelWaitSema and sceKernelSignalSema]

- [sceKernelWaitSema and sceKernelSignalSema](../../../core/libs/prx/libkernel/Semaphore/src/Semaphore.cpp) (libkernel) queue waiters in arrival order, or with `SCE_KERNEL_SEMA_ATTR_TH_PRIO` (0x02) by the priority the waiting thread has when it starts to wait, lower values first and equal ones in arrival order. `sceKernelSignalSema` hands its tokens to them in that order and stops at the first one that needs more than is left, so a later waiter that needs less keeps waiting, as a payload process on a PS5 (firmware 13.590.001) did with the attributes 1, 2 (at equal thread priority) and 0: a waiter of 3 queued before a waiter of 1 left both waiting after a signal of 1, a poll of 1 then still succeeded, and the later signal woke them in queue order. A `sceKernelWaitSema` or `sceKernelPollSema` that finds enough tokens takes them without queueing, as shadPS4's [semaphore](https://github.com/shadps4-emu/shadPS4/blob/06e813ff8c8e2a6d81b02cabfcc9543c3debf78e/src/core/libraries/kernel/threads/semaphore.cpp#L159-L206) does. A priority change while a thread waits does not move it in the queue, an attribute of 0 queues in arrival order, and the console's wake order for waiters of different priorities, and a `sceKernelWaitSema` that finds enough tokens while others are queued, are unverified
