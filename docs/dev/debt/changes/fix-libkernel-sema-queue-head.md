# libkernel

## change [sceKernelWaitSema and sceKernelSignalSema]

- [sceKernelWaitSema and sceKernelSignalSema](../../../core/libs/prx/libkernel/Semaphore/src/Semaphore.cpp) (libkernel) serve waiters in queue order: arrival order, or with `SCE_KERNEL_SEMA_ATTR_TH_PRIO` (0x02) the priority at the start of the wait. `sceKernelSignalSema` stops at the first waiter that needs more than is left, so a later waiter that needs less keeps waiting, as a payload process on a PS5 (firmware 13.590.001) measured for attributes 1, 2 and 0: a waiter of 3 queued before one of 1 left both waiting after a signal of 1, a poll of 1 still succeeded, and the later signal woke them in queue order. When the head times out, the console does not hand the token on: the waiter behind it stays asleep until its own timeout, and the token stays free (200 ms head, 3 s waiter, three attributes; infinite waits not measured). Unverified: wake order across priorities, priority changes while waiting, and `sceKernelWaitSema` taking tokens while others queue.
