# libc

## change [j1f and y1f]

- [j1f and y1f](../../../core/libs/prx/libc/src/J1f.cpp) (libc) - exception behaviour beyond `+-0` and finite negative arguments is unverified. They are FreeBSD msun's [`e_j1f.c` at `a737ef56ab`](https://github.com/freebsd/freebsd-src/blob/a737ef56ab3eb980f15326ed09b11a96b7c20f1c/lib/msun/src/e_j1f.c), the revision the `j0f` entry explains, calling this libc's msun `sinf`, `cosf`, `sqrtf` and `logf`. `y1f(+-0)` keeps the measured negative infinity with `errno` and exception flags unchanged. For finite negative arguments `y1f` returns the quiet NaN 0x7fc00000 without changing `errno` or raising invalid, as a payload process on a PS5 (firmware 13.590.001) measured for -1, -3.5 and -34.5, and checks infinities and NaNs first, so `-inf` gives `1 / (x + x * x)` as upstream does, a NaN with invalid on that console.

## change [j0f]

- [j0f](../../../core/libs/prx/libc/src/J0f.cpp) (libc) - is FreeBSD msun's [`e_j0f.c` at `a737ef56ab`](https://github.com/freebsd/freebsd-src/blob/a737ef56ab3eb980f15326ed09b11a96b7c20f1c/lib/msun/src/e_j0f.c) with this libc's msun float `sin`, `cos` and `sqrt`. A payload process on a PS5 (firmware 13.590.001) called `j0f`, `j1f`, `y0f` and `y1f` of the system `libSceLibcInternal.sprx` on 26 arguments each under MXCSR 0x1f80: compiled with fused multiply-adds, the `e_j0f.c` and `e_j1f.c` of `8192c85cb3` and `a737ef56ab` (which only adds inlining hints) give all 104 results, earlier revisions, release/10.0.0 included, miss four `y1f` results, and from `186f620727` on a negative argument to `y0f` or `y1f` gives 0xffc00000 where the console returns 0x7fc00000. Without fused multiply-adds no revision matches all 104, so that module fuses; this code does not, as the `libc.prx` most titles bundle does not (see `lgamma`), and the bundled `libc.prx` was not compared. Against release/10.0.0 the revision changes `j1f` on 2.5 to 7.6 and `y1f` across its range, about 8% of sampled arguments. `x*x` can raise overflow for large finite inputs. ±0 return 1, infinities return +0, NaNs propagate and `errno` is not set.

## change [y0f]

- [y0f](../../../core/libs/prx/libc/src/J0f.cpp) (libc) - differs from the console on 4 of the 26 measured arguments in the Mac x86_64 build: 0.5, 3.5 and 1.0000001 by 1 ulp, and `0x3f64c176` (about 0.893577, the first zero of y0) gives `0x32000000` where the console gives `0x325dd6a8`. The FMA note in `j0f` applies. It is FreeBSD msun's [`e_j0f.c` at `a737ef56ab`](https://github.com/freebsd/freebsd-src/blob/a737ef56ab3eb980f15326ed09b11a96b7c20f1c/lib/msun/src/e_j0f.c) with `j0f`'s P/Q tables and this libc's msun `logf`, `sinf`, `cosf` and `sqrtf`. The payload run that measured `j0f` (MXCSR 0x1f80, firmware 13.590.001) also called `y0f` on the same 26 arguments: `+-0` give -inf (0xff800000) with no flags raised, `+inf` gives +0, `-inf` gives 0xffc00000 with invalid, a NaN propagates, and -1, -3.5 and -34.5 give the quiet NaN 0x7fc00000 with no flags. `errno` stayed 0, and the other 22 values, with their flags, match.
