# shader

## change [2D accesses through a cube T#]

- [2D accesses through a cube T#](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Optimization/src/ResourceMaterializer.cpp) (#4139) read the T#'s `BASE_ARRAY` face as a cube access with face coordinate 0 does, measured on RDNA1 (gfx1010, RX 5700 XT) with the [hardware oracle](../HW_ORACLE.md) template run through the Windows driver's comgr and HIP runtime on 8x8 and 32x32 32_FLOAT cubes (`DEPTH` 5 and 11, `BASE_ARRAY` 0 and 6): `image_sample_lz`, plain `image_sample` (one level, and six levels in 2x2 compute quads) and `image_sample_l` (the third address VGPR is the LOD, integer and fractional) with DIM 2D equal DIM CUBE with face 0 on every row, with point and bilinear filters, wrap, clamp and mirror addressing and `DISABLE_CUBE_WRAP` 0 and 1, and the VGPR after the two coordinates is not read; `image_load` with DIM 2D reads texel (x, y) of the `BASE_ARRAY` face. So the face coordinates are cube coordinates in [1, 2]: the texel is `floor((u - 1) * width)`. They are translated through the cube mode DIM CUBE accesses use, which samples the face of the 2D-array view at (u - 1, v - 1) with the S# addressing. That matches the hardware inside the face, except within half a texel of its edges with a bilinear filter and `DISABLE_CUBE_WRAP` 0, where the hardware blends texels of the neighbouring faces (also measured on RDNA2, gfx1035, 8x8 cubes with `BASE_ARRAY` 0 and 6). Outside it the hardware clamps a coordinate below 1 to the first texel under every filter, wrap mode and `DISABLE_CUBE_WRAP` setting measured, clamps a coordinate of 2 or more to the last texel when `DISABLE_CUBE_WRAP` is 0 (point filter) and applies the S# addressing to it when it is 1; the translation does none of this, as for DIM CUBE. Only `image_sample_lz`, plain `image_sample`, `image_sample_l`, mip-less `image_load`, and mip-less `image_store` to an image the program only stores to take this mode. Such a store writes texel (x, y) of the `BASE_ARRAY` face, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036, [by simonbbby](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/6799#issuecomment-6217609589), not the PS5's gfx1013) with the [hardware oracle](../HW_ORACLE.md) on 8x8 mip-less cube arrays (`DEPTH` 11) of 32_UINT, 32_FLOAT and 16_16_16_16_FLOAT ([the last also by simonbbby](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/6799#issuecomment-6224023050), with exactly representable half values) with `BASE_ARRAY` 0 and 6, wave32 and wave64: the 64 stored texels land on that face and every other byte of the 64 KiB backing is unchanged. Other formats are measured on gfx1036 (wave32, driver 31.0.22044.1): `8_8_8_8_UNORM`, `8_8_8_8_SNORM`, `16_16_FLOAT` and `32_SINT` stores at `BASE_ARRAY` 0 and 6 land on that face and change no other byte of the 64 KiB backing. At `BASE_ARRAY` 0, a `dmask:0x1` store to `8_8_8_8_UNORM` or `16_16_FLOAT` writes zero to the components outside the mask, and a `32_SINT` store writes the data register's dword unconverted: a value of 1.0 stores `0x3f800000`, not 1. Neither gfx1013 nor Crimson Desert's own 256x256 tiled descriptor is measured. Other DIM 2D accesses through a cube T# (gathers, bias, comparison, offsets, derivatives, atomics, stores to an image the program also reads, stores of a format written through a conversion other than the 32_SINT, 16_16_FLOAT and 16_16_16_16_FLOAT `dmask:0x1` stores above (every other such format is not measured), mip loads and stores) still throw.
