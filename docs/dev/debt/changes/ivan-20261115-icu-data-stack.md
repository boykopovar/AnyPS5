# libSceIcu

## change [Additional callable exports]

- [Additional callable exports](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Export.cpp) - 34 remaining throwing functions match NIDs in the public 3.20 catalogue and both version-labelled images; image labels do not authenticate retail firmware provenance. Their prototypes and behavior are unverified and retain `NotImplemented_nid_no_patch`. The 14 `ubrk_*` and two `udata_set*` exports moved to [BreakIterator.cpp](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/BreakIterator.cpp) and [Data.cpp](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Data.cpp). Existing locale, character, normalization and property-map implementations remain outside this throwing inventory.

## change named stubs declared in [Identified.hpp]

- The 21 parameterless throwing stubs declared in [Identified.hpp](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Identified.hpp) carry the ICU C API names whose `tools/nid_names.py` hash equals the inventory NID; the names were taken from the identifiers of the ICU 78.1 `common`, `i18n` and `io` sources. A name match does not show that the console function has ICU's prototype or behavior. Most of them read resource, converter or character-name data that no host library provides.

## change [Break iterator factories]

- [Break iterator factories](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Export.cpp) - Four 3.20 callable NIDs are the ICU 68 `BreakIterator::createCharacterInstance`, `createWordInstance`, `createLineInstance` and `createSentenceInstance(const Locale&, UErrorCode&)`: their `icu_68` mangled names hash to these NIDs, and the selector each wrapper passes is the `UBreakIteratorType` that ICU 68 passes to `BreakIterator::createInstance`. They are declared as throwing placeholders; their ICU 68 C++ prototypes/layouts remain unverified despite the source-built ICU 78 rule data. The four selector wrappers have identical instruction bytes in the 3.20 and later version-labelled modules, and corresponding consumer switch cases call them. Their NIDs and internal layouts differ, and the later NIDs match no `icu_60` to `icu_79` name of these functions; no forwarding or binary compatibility is claimed. Later image version metadata does not authenticate a retail firmware release.

## change [Cleanup]

- [Cleanup](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Configuration.cpp) is unverified on a console. `u_cleanup` follows ICU 78.1 [ucln_cmn.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/049e0d6a420629ac7db77256987d083a563287b5/icu4c/source/common/ucln_cmn.cpp) for the state this module keeps: it clears the functions stored by `u_setMemoryFunctions` and resets the data and time zone files directories. Pointers returned earlier by the directory getters are invalidated, as in ICU. It first calls the native ICU 78.1 `u_cleanup`, whose cleanup chains release the backend's shared caches (see Allocator integration). The NID comes from the 3.20 inventory and its presence on current firmware is unproven (see ICU export availability). Closes when: compared with the console.

## change [Memory function configuration]

- [Memory function configuration](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Configuration.cpp) is unverified on a console. `u_setMemoryFunctions` follows ICU 78.1 [cmemory.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/049e0d6a420629ac7db77256987d083a563287b5/icu4c/source/common/cmemory.cpp): it stores the context and callbacks under a lock and rejects a null callback with `U_ILLEGAL_ARGUMENT_ERROR`, and native thunks route the ICU 78.1 backend's allocations through them (see Allocator integration); their guest calling convention is unverified on the console. Firmware 13.42 is reported to export no `u_setMemoryFunctions` ([#11132](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/11132)); the NID comes from the 3.20 inventory (see ICU export availability). Closes when: compared with the console.

## change [Character property]

- [Character property](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Properties.cpp) and [case mapping](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/CaseMapping.cpp) exports retain their #13578 `NotImplemented_nid_no_patch` guards for data-dependent inputs despite the filtered package: `u_hasBinaryProperty` and `u_getIntPropertyValue` for emoji, NFKC, NFKC_Casefold, Indic positional and syllabic category and vertical orientation properties, `u_getIntPropertyMaxValue` for the last three, and `u_strToLower` and `u_strToUpper` with a null (default) locale. Prototypes follow the ICU 78.1 headers; other inputs follow its compiled-in tables from the [3rdparty/icu](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/tree/049e0d6a4206/icu4c/source/common) submodule; null pointers ICU dereferences and lengths below -1 throw `std::invalid_argument`. Closes when: compared with the console and the data files are available.

## change [`u_getIntPropertyMap`]

- [`u_getIntPropertyMap`](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Properties.cpp) forwards integer-property map lookups to bundled ICU 78.1; its NID name comes from the recovered inventory, while the prototype follows ICU's public header. Its existing guards for maps backed by ICU data files remain, even with the source-built package. The map uses Unicode 17.0 property data and has not been checked on a console.

## change [Locale writing direction]

- [Locale writing direction](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/RightToLeft.cpp) is unverified on a console. `uloc_isRightToLeft` throws for a null locale (ICU uses the default locale) and for a locale with no script whose language is not one of the 17 that ICU 78.1 [loclikely.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/049e0d6a420629ac7db77256987d083a563287b5/icu4c/source/common/loclikely.cpp#L348-L386) lists: the existing likely-subtags guard remains even with the source-built data package. Otherwise it follows that function, with the script direction from the ICU submodule. The NID is the ICU 68 name; [ICU 68.2](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/84e1f26ea77152936e70d53178a816dbfbf69989/icu4c/source/common/loclikely.cpp#L1301-L1348) returns false for a language of eight or more letters without a script, which throws here, and for the scripts `Ougr`, `Gara` and `Sidt`, which return true here. Closes when: compared with the console and the likely subtags data is available.

## add Functional

- [Source-built data](../../../cmake/IcuData.cmake) for [libSceIcu](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Data.cpp) has unverified console/data-version equivalence. Pinned ICU 78.1 host tools build one `icudt78_dat` package. The [filter](../../../cmake/IcuDataFilter.json) retains break rules, dictionaries, break locales, availability and parent/alias resources, normalization, converter aliases, layout, emoji and misc data. LSTM/AdaBoost models are excluded; dictionary segmentation is the fallback and console model selection is unverified. Other locale trees, language, region, units, collation, rule-based-number and transliteration resources remain excluded. Guest file I/O stays disabled. Closes when: compared with console data.

- [Native host tools](../../../cmake/icu-host/CMakeLists.txt) for [libSceIcu](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/CMakeLists.txt) use a separate CMake build even for cross builds. They run on Linux (GCC 15) and Windows (MinGW GCC 15.2, Python 3.12); macOS execution remains unverified. Closes when: tested on macOS.

- [C break iterators](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/BreakIterator.cpp) have unverified console ABI and behavior. The 14 `ubrk_*` exports forward to pinned ICU 78.1 [ubrk.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/049e0d6a420629ac7db77256987d083a563287b5/icu4c/source/common/ubrk.cpp); guest `UChar` is 16-bit, `UBool` 8-bit and status 32-bit. ICU retains the caller's UTF-16 buffer without copying; it must remain alive and unchanged until replacement or close. Handles belong to this backend and cannot be mixed with ICU 68 objects. Null handles or status pointers throw where ICU dereferences them; negative locale indices and index equal to the count throw where `locavailable.cpp` reads outside its allocated array; other arguments and status values pass through ICU. UText callbacks, rule construction, cloning and locale-query exports remain unavailable. Closes when: compared with the console.

- [Data registration](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Data.cpp) has unverified console behavior. The two `udata_set*` setters follow ICU 78.1 [udata.cpp](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/049e0d6a420629ac7db77256987d083a563287b5/icu4c/source/common/udata.cpp). Common data must be correctly aligned and remain alive while ICU uses it; ICU validates the header, not the extent of caller storage. A null status is a no-op for common-data registration and is ignored by file-access configuration. Closes when: compared with the console.

- [Allocator integration](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/Configuration.cpp) is unverified on the console. `u_setMemoryFunctions` and `u_cleanup` keep the validation and state described above and also forward to the native ICU 78.1 functions. Native callback thunks invoke the configured guest-ABI allocator for ICU allocation, reallocation and freeing. Close releases per-handle allocations; shared caches survive until native `u_cleanup`, which runs before clearing the callbacks. Reconfiguration and cleanup require no concurrent use or outstanding handles, as in ICU. Hand-ported objects retain their own allocation paths. Firmware availability remains as recorded under ICU export availability; no ICU 68 C++ objects are changed. Closes when: compared with the console, including the guest callbacks on Windows.
