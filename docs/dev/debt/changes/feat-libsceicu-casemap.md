- [CaseMap::toLower, toUpper and fold](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/CaseMapping.cpp) (libSceIcu) - differ from ICU 68.2 for characters assigned or given a case mapping after Unicode 13 (U+019B, U+0264) and in Greek uppercasing ([78.1](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/049e0d6a420629ac7db77256987d083a563287b5/icu4c/source/common/ustrcase.cpp#L1131-L1175)): 68.2 writes a disjunctive eta with a precomposed accent (ή, Ή, ἢ) as U+0397 U+0301, where 78.1 writes U+0389, and a dialytika added after a combining accent as a precomposed letter, where 78.1 writes U+0308.
- [CaseMap::toLower, toUpper and fold](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceIcu/CaseMapping.cpp) (libSceIcu) - differ from ICU 68.2 in every locale around older characters whose Cased or Case_Ignorable property changed after Unicode 13 (U+0295, U+1734, U+1171E), which moves the final sigma: the sigma of A Σ U+0295, U+0295 Σ, A U+1734 Σ and A U+1171E Σ lowercases to U+03C3, U+03C2, U+03C2 and U+03C2 in 68.2, and to U+03C2, U+03C3, U+03C3 and U+03C3 here. In `el` uppercasing, ή U+0295 gives U+0397 U+0295 in 68.2 and U+0389 U+0295 here.
