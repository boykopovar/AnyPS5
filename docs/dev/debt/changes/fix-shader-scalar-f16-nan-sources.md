# shader

## change [Signaling NaN inputs]

- [Signaling NaN inputs](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/FloatInstructions.cpp) of the f32 and f16 unary instructions (`v_sqrt/rcp/rsq/log/exp/sin/cos/fract/floor/ceil/trunc/rndne/rcp_iflag/frexp_mant`), `v_ldexp_f32/f16`, `v_frexp_mant_f16`, `v_div_fixup_f32/f16` and `v_mullit_f32` are returned quieted only with IEEE=1 and unchanged with IEEE=0, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1035) in both modes, and so are those of the f64 forms (`v_sqrt/rcp/rsq/fract/floor/ceil/trunc/rndne/frexp_mant/ldexp/div_fixup_f64`) and `v_cvt_f64_f32`, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036) in both modes; shaders without a known float mode follow IEEE=0. `v_pack_b32_f16` quiets each signaling NaN half by the same rule, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036) in both modes. A signaling NaN result of `v_cubesc/cubetc/cubema_f32` is quieted by the same rule and keeps the sign the instruction gives it, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036) in both modes. `v_add/sub/subrev/mul/fma/fmac/fmamk/fmaak/mul_legacy_f32` and `v_fma_mix_f32` with f32 sources choose their NaN result on the bits, measured over 1000 operand triples in both modes: the first NaN source in operand order after its modifiers (the subtrahend negated, as the hardware adds its negation), quieted only with IEEE=1, or the default NaN 0xffc00000 for an invalid operation; in the fma forms an invalid product (inf * 0) gives the default NaN even when src2 is a NaN, and in the legacy forms a zero factor makes the product 0 even when the other factor is a NaN (a NaN src2 still wins). `v_mad_legacy_f32` and `v_mac_legacy_f32` follow the same rule with their separately rounded product, as measured on gfx1010 (#1281, #1290): a NaN source quieted only with IEEE=1, inf - inf giving 0xffc00000, the zero rule hiding a NaN factor and a NaN src2 winning. `v_add_f64`, `v_mul_f64`, `v_fma_f64` and `v_div_fmas_f64` follow the same rule with the default NaN 0xfff8000000000000, an inf * 0 product winning over a NaN src2, measured on RDNA2 (gfx1031) in both modes. `v_add/sub/subrev/mul/fma/fmac/fmamk/fmaak_f16` follow the same rule on the bits with the default NaN 0xfe00, in the native f16 path and in the f32 lowering: `v_sub_f16` negates src1 and `v_subrev_f16` src0, and src0 is still taken first. Measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036, Ryzen 9 7950X iGPU, AMD Windows driver 32.0.21042.62 through HIP) at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1, with `DX10_CLAMP` 0 and 1 and f16 denormals kept and flushed: all 7260 NaN results of the VOP2 and VOP3 forms of the first six over 1760 operand rows each, and 23 rows of all eight with neg, abs and clamp modifiers (`agc_driver_float16_nan_sources`). The PS5's gfx1013 is not measured. `v_fma_mix_f32` with f16 sources and the separately rounded mad/mac forms (not measurable on RDNA2) follow the same rule on hardware but their NaN bits are left to the driver, except that an invalid operation of `v_fma_mix_f32` with f16 sources (an inf * 0 product, also with a NaN src2, or a NaN from sources that are not NaN) gives the default NaN 0xffc00000, which NVIDIA does not return on its own. Measured on RDNA2 (gfx1031, Radeon RX 6700 XT, AMD Windows driver 32.0.21045.5002 through HIP) at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1 with f16 and f32 denormals kept and flushed, over six inf * 0 products with eight NaN src2 values in every f16 fma form (#7074); the AMD Windows driver 32.0.21042.62 on an RDNA2 iGPU (Ryzen 5 7600) instead returns src2 quieted through Vulkan in the f16 fma forms. `v_cvt_f16_f32`, `v_cvt_f32_f64`, `v_dot2_f32_f16`, `v_fma_mixlo_f16` and `v_fma_mixhi_f16` always quiet and `v_div_scale_f32` never does: the f32 signaling NaN 0x7f812345 as src0 or src2 gives 0x7e09 from mixlo and mixhi at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1 on RDNA2 (gfx1036), where `v_fma_mix_f32` gives 0x7f812345 at IEEE=0 and 0x7fc12345 at IEEE=1. The payload of an f16 NaN source of mixlo and mixhi is left to the driver's f16 to f32 conversion, which NVIDIA replaces.

## change [f16 NaN results with IEEE_MODE clear]

- [f16 NaN results with IEEE_MODE clear](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/FloatInstructions.cpp) are the device's when the startup probe (`ProbeFloat16HostNans`) finds them equal to the selected ones above: those of the native `v_pk_add_f16`, `v_pk_mul_f16`, `v_pk_fma_f16` and `v_pk_fmac_f16`, whose second operand is replaced by the first when the first is a NaN (a driver may swap the operands), and of the native scalar `v_add_f16`, `v_sub_f16`, `v_subrev_f16`, `v_mul_f16`, `v_fma_f16`, `v_fmac_f16`, `v_fmamk_f16` and `v_fmaak_f16`, whose second source is replaced the same way by the first (in `v_subrev_f16`, src1 by the negated src0). The probe runs the packed forms and the scalar fma forms; the scalar add, sub, subrev and mul forms use the same native f16 vector instructions and are not probed. The probe compares 4096 operand triples of 16 special halves in both lanes, with and without `op_sel`, `neg` and clamp, without FLOAT_MODE and in two FP16_OVFL modes, through a compute shader; the graphics stages rely on the same device behaviour. IEEE_MODE=1 keeps the selection (a Radeon RX 7900 XTX returns a signalling NaN source unchanged there, where RDNA quiets it), and so does a literal NaN source, which a driver may fold. `APS5_NO_HOST_F16_NANS` skips the probe.

## change [Scalar f16 add, sub, subrev and mul denormals]

- [Scalar f16 add, sub, subrev and mul denormals](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/FloatInstructions.cpp) follow the `FLOAT_MODE` f16 denormal field at nearest-even: bit 0 preserves inputs and bit 1 preserves the result; otherwise a denormal is flushed to its signed zero. Measured on RDNA2 (gfx1036) with IEEE=0/1 and FP16_OVFL=0/1, over 32 operand pairs in all four denormal modes, including signed zeros, denormals, minimum normals, infinities and NaNs. Multiply detects tininess after rounding to 11 significant bits with an unbounded exponent, before gradual half packing: `0x3bff * 0x0400` flushes, while `0x3c01 * 0x03ff` rounds to `0x0400` and is kept. `v_fma_f16`, `v_fmac_f16`, `v_fmamk_f16` and `v_fmaak_f16` follow the same field at nearest-even: inputs are flushed when bit 0 is clear, and the result is flushed when bit 1 is clear if the exact product-sum rounded to 11 significant bits with an unbounded exponent is below 2^-14, so `0x0400 * 0x0400 + 0x8400` rounds to `0x8400` and is kept. Measured on RDNA2 (gfx1031, Radeon RX 6700 XT, AMD Windows driver 32.0.21045.5002 through HIP) at IEEE=0 and IEEE=1 with denorm16 0..3 over 5075 operand triples of `v_fma_f16` and `v_fmac_f16` (4000 from 20 halves around the denormal range, 75 whose product-sum lies just below 2^-14 and 1000 random), and over the first 4075 of them for `v_fmamk_f16` and `v_fmaak_f16`, FP16_OVFL=1, the clamp, neg and abs modifiers and op_sel. Directed rounding and `v_fma_mixlo/mixhi_f16` keep their previous denormal lowering. Shaders without a known float mode keep the previous lowering. Scalar `v_sub_f16`/`v_subrev_f16` share `float16Binary` with add/mul and were measured on gfx1036 over the same operand set as add (1156 non-NaN-input rows per mode); their NaN results follow the signaling NaN entry above.
