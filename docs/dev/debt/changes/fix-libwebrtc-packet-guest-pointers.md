# libwebrtc

## change rtc::CopyOnWriteBuffer

- [rtc::CopyOnWriteBuffer](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/CopyOnWriteBuffer.cpp) (libwebrtc) - console layout and allocator choice are unverified. Five exports follow [copy_on_write_buffer.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/rtc_base/copy_on_write_buffer.cc), buffer.h, ref_counted_object.h, ref_counter.h and api/scoped_refptr.h (agreeing at M127): 24-byte object and 32-byte `FinalRefCountedObject<Buffer>` backing with atomic refcount at 24. Guest scalar new/delete allocate the backing and guest array new/delete own uninitialized payload bytes (`ApplicationHeapFree` handles both sized and unsized scalar delete). Guest `CopyOnWriteBuffer` objects, backings, and unshare payload spans are probed, read, validated, and written in 4096-byte chunks through `GuestPtr`, `GuestBytes`, and `GuestWritable_nid_no_patch`. Null or inaccessible objects, invalid backings, and unsharing null or unreadable backing throw `std::invalid_argument`. Closes when: compared with the console.

## change rtc::RateTracker

- [rtc::RateTracker](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/RateTracker.cpp) (libwebrtc) - layout and dispatch are unverified on console. Four exports follow [rate_tracker.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/rtc_base/rate_tracker.cc) (byte-identical at M127): 64-byte Itanium layout, guest array new/delete, circular buckets, rounded partial buckets, and initialization warm-up. Guest tracker objects, vtable slots, `bucketCount` bounds, `(bucketCount + 1) * 8`-byte bucket array ranges, and bucket entries are validated, read, and updated through `GuestPtr` and `GuestWritable_nid_no_patch`. Invalid constructor parameters, null, out-of-range, or inaccessible trackers, vtables or bucket arrays and array size overflow throw. A local D1/D0/Time vtable uses guest ABI calls and `TimeMillis`; RTTI is unsupported (null typeinfo). Closes when: compared with the console.

## change webrtc::PacketOptions constructors

- [webrtc::PacketOptions constructors](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/PacketOptions.cpp) (libwebrtc) - nothing is checked on the console. Adapted from [transport.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/api/call/transport.cc) and [transport.h](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/api/call/transport.h): 16 bytes, `packet_id` at 0 and six flags at 8 to 13, copied and written through `GuestPtr` and `GuestWritable_nid_no_patch`. Default sets `packet_id` to -1, `is_media` true and the other flags false; copy copies all seven fields. M127-M131 have `is_retransmit` false at 8 instead, and M133 adds `send_as_ect1` at 11; M132-M134 give the same field values. Null or inaccessible objects or sources throw `std::invalid_argument`. Closes when: compared with the console.

## change rtc::SentPacket constructors

- [rtc::SentPacket constructors](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/SentPacket.cpp) (libwebrtc) - console revision and layout are unverified. Adapted from M134 [sent_packet.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/rtc_base/network/sent_packet.cc) and its header: `PacketInfo` is 40 bytes (enums, optional `uint16_t` at 12, sizes at 16/24/32) and `SentPacket` is 56 bytes (signed IDs at 0/8, `info` at 16), copied and written through `GuestPtr` and `GuestWritable_nid_no_patch`. Both M127 and M134 give these offsets, with M134 adding `is_media` at 2 (padding in M127); default sets false flags, unknown enums, no network ID, and zero sizes, and copy preserves engaged metadata. Null or inaccessible objects or sources throw `std::invalid_argument`. Closes when: revision and layout are compared with the console.

## change webrtc::callback_list_impl::CallbackListReceivers constructor

Closes when: compared with the console.

## change rtc::KeyParams constructor

- [rtc::KeyParams constructor](../../../core/libs/prx/libwebrtc/src/KeyParams.cpp) (libwebrtc) - `KT_RSA` throws through `NotImplemented_nid_no_patch`: it stores `kRsaDefaultModSize`, 1024 at M127 and 2048 at M134, and the module's revision is not identified. A type other than `KT_RSA` or `KT_ECDSA` throws where upstream only `RTC_DCHECK_NOTREACHED`s and leaves the object unset. Adapted from [ssl_identity.cc](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/8d78f5de6c27b2c793039989ea381f1428fb0100/rtc_base/ssl_identity.cc), whose WebRTC notice and BSD licence head the file: `KT_ECDSA` checks `GuestWritable_nid_no_patch` and stores `type_` 1 and `EC_NIST_P256` through `GuestPtr` in the 12-byte layout of `ssl_identity.h`. A null or inaccessible object throws `std::invalid_argument`. Closes when: the revision is identified and compared with the console.
