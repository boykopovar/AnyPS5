# libSceLibreSsl3
## change [OpenSSL_version]
- [OpenSSL_version](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceLibreSsl3/Export.cpp) - other firmware revisions are unverified. Selectors 0 through 5 follow the public firmware 13.40 measurements in [discussion #8981](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/discussions/8981#discussioncomment-19111214); unknown selectors follow LibreSSL 4.0.0 and return `not available`. A PS5 (firmware 11.60, payload process; a title process may differ) returned `LibreSSL 3.7.3` for selector 0, not the `LibreSSL 4.0.0` returned here, so the version text depends on the firmware; selectors 1 to 5 returned the texts above, and 6, 7, 8, 100, -1, `INT_MAX` and `INT_MIN` returned `not available`, each as the same pointer on two calls. `dlsym` found `OpenSSL_version` in `libSceLibreSsl3.sprx`, and did not under its bare name in `libSceLibreSsl.sprx`.
## change [ERR_load_crypto_strings and ERR_peek_last_error]
- [ERR_load_crypto_strings and ERR_peek_last_error](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceLibreSsl3/Export.cpp) have no console execution verification. [Firmware 13.42 modules run on a PC](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/9535#issuecomment-6407060914) retained numeric-only output after loaders and matched queue order/overflow; the two firmware modules do not share a queue: on a PS5 (firmware 11.60, payload process; a title process may differ), after `ASN1_get_object` errors the `ERR_peek_error` and `ERR_get_error` of `libSceLibreSsl3` returned `0x0DFFF09B` where those of `libSceLibreSsl` returned 0, and after `ERR_clear_error` both peeks returned 0. This implementation reads libSceLibreSsl's calling-thread queue.
## change [ASN1_get_object]
- [ASN1_get_object](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceLibreSsl3/src/IntegerDecoder.cpp) was probed on a PS5 (firmware 11.60, payload process; a title process may differ) with 25 header and bound cases and one null-pointer case. Its 64-bit lengths and BER header rules follow LibreSSL 4.0.0 [asn1_old_lib.c](https://github.com/libressl/openbsd/blob/ce488b78e0bd994780569df718596b9776ff681a/src/lib/libcrypto/asn1/asn1_old_lib.c) and [asn1_lib.c](https://github.com/libressl/openbsd/blob/ce488b78e0bd994780569df718596b9776ff681a/src/lib/libcrypto/asn1/asn1_lib.c). The console agreed with these rules in 23 of the 25 cases (definite and indefinite lengths, long forms, high tags, truncated headers, lengths past the bound). It accepted a negative bound (`-1` and `INT64_MIN` with `30 03 02 01 05` returned `0x20` and advanced the pointer by 2), and so does this, for any negative bound (only these two were measured), and a null tag pointer raised SIGSEGV, where this throws without mutation; the other null arguments were not tried. Its errors went to the queue of `libSceLibreSsl3`, which `libSceLibreSsl` does not see, while this uses the parent-library queue.
