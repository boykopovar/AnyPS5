# relinker

## change lowers what Rosetta lacks on top of

- `--to-rosetta` lowers what Rosetta lacks on top of `--to-intel` ([matcher](../../../core/relinker/codegen/src/x86/Amd64OnlyInstructionMatcher.cpp)). AVX2, FMA, BMI1/2, F16C and MOVBE were observed to execute on an M5 Max without being reported through CPUID; this is not a guarantee for other Rosetta hosts. ANDN and RDRAND fault on an M1 running macOS 15.3.1 and execute on an M1 running macOS 26.6.2, so on the M1 the difference follows the macOS version; on that host BEXTR, BLSI, BLSMSK, BLSR, TZCNT, LZCNT, POPCNT, BZHI, MULX, PDEP, PEXT, RORX, SARX, SHLX, SHRX, MOVBE, FMA, AVX2 and F16C execute as well, its CPUID leaf 1 reports none of MOVBE, FMA, AVX or F16C, and RDSEED, RDPID, CLWB, ADCX and ADOX raise SIGILL. They also execute on an M5 running macOS 26.4, where the rest of BMI1 and BMI2, LZCNT, PREFETCHW, CLFLUSHOPT, AVX, AVX2, FMA, F16C and MOVBE execute as well although CPUID leaf 7 reads zero and leaves 1 and 0x80000001 report none of them, and where RDSEED, CLWB, RDPID, ADCX, ADOX, SHA and AVX-512F raise an illegal instruction. On that host the BMI1 and BMI2 results match the Intel SDM definitions over 200,000 random inputs each for PDEP, PEXT, BEXTR, BZHI, LZCNT and TZCNT, so they compute the documented result and do not only avoid a fault. ANDN, BLSI, BLSMSK, BLSR, SHLX, SHRX, SARX, RORX, BZHI, BEXTR, MULX, PDEP and PEXT are lowered, but RDRAND and the other BMI instructions still depend on the host. RDSEED becomes RDRAND and CLWB a NOP. RDPID returns processor 0, and RDTSCP runs unchanged with ECX set to 0 after it, because Rosetta's TSC_AUX is the constant 0xAAAAAAAB (also measured on an M5, macOS 27.0.1). An RDPID or RDTSCP site with no movable instruction after it for the jump is kept; a kept RDTSCP reports 0xAAAAAAAB. ADCX/ADOX fault under Rosetta and are not converted: compilers do not emit them, and code that checks CPUID sees no ADX. The conversions are covered by the converter tests. ANDN lowering is also executed by an x86-64 test build under Rosetta on the M1; the other conversions were originally checked on an M5 Max, outside this repository's CI.

## change output faults on an M2 running macOS 14.8.9

- `--to-rosetta` output faults on an M2 running macOS 14.8.9 when it reaches AVX, AVX2, FMA, F16C, RDRAND or a BMI1/BMI2 instruction other than ANDN, BLSI, BLSMSK, BLSR, SHLX, SHRX, SARX, RORX, BZHI, BEXTR, MULX, PDEP, PEXT, TZCNT and LZCNT: Rosetta on that host raises SIGILL for them and for RDSEED, RDPID, CLWB, ADCX and ADOX ([matcher](../../../core/relinker/codegen/src/x86/Amd64OnlyInstructionMatcher.cpp)). Only TZCNT, LZCNT, POPCNT, MOVBE, CLFLUSHOPT, PREFETCHW and RDTSCP execute, and RDTSCP sets ECX to 0xAAAAAAAB. CPUID leaf 1 reports POPCNT and none of MOVBE, AVX, FMA, F16C or RDRAND, leaf 7 reads zero, and leaf 0x80000001 reports RDTSCP but neither LZCNT nor PREFETCHW. An M2 Max on macOS 27.2 and an M2 Pro on macOS 27.0.1 report the same CPUID but execute everything except RDSEED, RDPID, CLWB, ADCX and ADOX, so the faults follow the macOS version, not the chip.

## change [BLSI, BLSMSK and BLSR lowering]

- [BLSI, BLSMSK and BLSR lowering](../../../core/relinker/codegen/src/x86/BlsLowering.cpp) uses scalar LEA, AND, XOR, NEG and OR after a TEST on the source, with two scratch registers saved below the red zone, under `--to-rosetta`, following their SDM entries: SF follows the result, ZF follows the result for BLSI and BLSR and clears for BLSMSK, CF is set for a zero source (BLSR, BLSMSK) or a nonzero source (BLSI) through STC on the branch that needs it, OF clears, and AF and PF are undefined. A 64-bit stack-relative displacement that exceeds signed disp32 after reserving the spill frame throws, as for ANDN. The execution test runs only on an x86-64 host and checks the SDM semantics, not the hardware instruction; precise fault delivery inside a spill sequence has not been verified.

## change [SHLX, SHRX, SARX and RORX lowering]

- [SHLX, SHRX, SARX and RORX lowering](../../../core/relinker/codegen/src/x86/ShiftLowering.cpp) uses `shl`, `shr`, `sar` by CL and `ror` by immediate under `--to-rosetta`, with the count masked to the operand size as the BMI2 forms do and RCX spilled in a 160-byte frame below the red zone. The BMI2 forms leave RFLAGS unchanged, so the stub saves them with `pushfq` and `popfq`, unless the next instructions, through at most 16 MOV, MOVZX, MOVSX, LEA, PUSH, POP, NOP or BMI2 shifts, reach an ADD, OR, AND, SUB, XOR, CMP, TEST or NEG; a fault in those instructions then sees the flags the scalar shift left. A stack-relative displacement over signed disp32 after the frame throws. The execution test runs natively or under Rosetta against the SDM result and the flags loaded before the stub, not the hardware instruction; precise fault delivery inside a spill sequence has not been verified.

## change [BZHI and BEXTR lowering]

- [BZHI and BEXTR lowering](../../../core/relinker/codegen/src/x86/BitfieldLowering.cpp) builds the mask with `shl` by CL and `not` on a scratch register and applies it with `and` under `--to-rosetta`, with RCX spilled in a 160-byte frame below the red zone. An index or start at or past the operand size takes the SDM's unmasked (BZHI) or zero (BEXTR) branch, not a scalar shift's masked count. BZHI sets CF through STC there, and ZF and SF follow the result; BEXTR clears CF and OF, ZF follows the result, and its SF, undefined in the SDM, follows the result here. A 64-bit stack-relative displacement that exceeds signed disp32 after reserving the frame throws, as for ANDN. The execution test runs natively on x86-64 or under Rosetta and checks result and flags against an SDM model that matched a Zen 2's BZHI and BEXTR on 122,880 cases, not against the hardware instruction in the same run; precise fault delivery inside a spill sequence has not been verified.

## change applies the BMI1, BMI2, CLWB and RDPID lowerings

- `--to-avx` ([matcher](../../../core/relinker/codegen/src/x86/Amd64OnlyInstructionMatcher.cpp)) applies the BMI1, BMI2, CLWB and RDPID lowerings of `--to-rosetta` and adds TZCNT, LZCNT and MOVBE, for hosts with AVX but without AVX2, such as the Sandy Bridge and Ivy Bridge CPUs of 2011 and 2012 Macs. AVX2, F16C, FMA, RDRAND and RDSEED are not lowered, so a title that reaches one of them still raises an illegal instruction; Sandy Bridge has neither RDRAND nor RDSEED, so the `--to-rosetta` RDSEED-to-RDRAND rewrite would not help there. It requires `--macos`: the Linux and Windows runtimes stop at start-up when the host lacks BMI1, BMI2, LZCNT, MOVBE or AVX2 and do not yet read which of them a conversion lowered.

## add Functional

- [PDEP and PEXT lowering](../../../core/relinker/codegen/src/x86/DepositExtractLowering.cpp) under `--to-rosetta` and `--to-avx` runs one loop iteration of up to 8 (PDEP) or 9 (PEXT) scalar instructions per set mask bit, up to 64 iterations; no title has been measured with it. RFLAGS, which both instructions leave unchanged, is always saved with PUSHFQ, even when the next instructions overwrite it, with four or five scratch registers in a 192-byte frame below the red zone. A stack-relative displacement over signed disp32 after the frame throws, as for ANDN. The execution test checks the result, the other registers, RFLAGS and the red zone against the definitions in [Intel SDM volume 2B](https://cdrdv2-public.intel.com/812384/253667-sdm-vol-2b.pdf) and, on a host with BMI2, against the hardware instruction on 200,000 random inputs; it has not been run under Rosetta or on a CPU without BMI2, and precise fault delivery inside the stub has not been verified.
