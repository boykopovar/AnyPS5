# shader

## change [v_fma_f64 and v_div_fmas_f64 denormals]

- [v_fma_f64 and v_div_fmas_f64 denormals](../../../core/shader/recompiler/Translation/src/Float64Instructions.cpp) follow the `FLOAT_MODE` f16/f64 denormal field, as measured on RDNA3 (gfx1101, RX 7700 XT); the PS5's gfx1013 is not measured. `v_fma_f64` flushes denormal operands to a zero of their sign when bit 0 is clear (0 and 2); `v_div_fmas_f64` keeps them. When bit 1 is clear (0 and 1) both flush a result that rounds to 53 significant bits below 2^-1022 with unbounded exponent, to a zero of its sign, `v_div_fmas_f64` after its VCC scale by 2^±128. On RDNA2 (gfx1036), IEEE=0/1, denorm16 0..3, a product rounding up to 2^-1022 is kept in all four (`0x2000000000000001 * 0x1ffffffffffffffe`, 2^-1022 - 2^-1126), while the exact tie `0x3fefffffffffffff * 0x0010000000000000` (2^-1022 - 2^-1075) flushes in 0 and 1, kept in 2 and 3; `v_div_fmas_f64` matches. RDNA2 (gfx1035) matches in 0 and 3 (3,000 rows each). Also 300 random biased triples. `v_fma_f64` also follows the f16/f64 round field, as measured on RDNA2 (gfx1031): rounding 1 to 3 rounds the exact `a * b + c` once, up, down or toward zero. An overflow gives infinity on the side the mode rounds toward and the largest finite f64 on the other, a sum that cancels exactly is -0 only when rounding down, and the result flush above tests the value rounded in that mode. The directed modes are computed with 64-bit integers ([f64_fma_round](../../../core/shader/recompiler/SpirvBackend/src/SpirvAlu/SpirvAluEmitterFloat64.cpp)), which costs more than the native operation and needs the Int64 capability. Measured with [tools/hw-oracle](../HW_ORACLE.md) on an RX 6750 XT at IEEE=0 and 1, denorm16 0..3 and round16 0..3 over 17,920 triples, with no mismatches. `v_div_fmas_f64` is not measured in the directed modes and still rounds to nearest there. Without a known float mode, lower as before.
