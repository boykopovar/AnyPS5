# libSceFreeTypeOl technical debt

### Known export names; ABI prototypes unverified

- The 92 `sceFreeTypeOl*_nid_no_patch` exports in [Export.cpp](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceFreeTypeOl/Export.cpp) are present in the PS5 3.20 export inventory from [PS5-3.20_Libs](https://github.com/DNNDHH/PS5-3.20_Libs). The [verified NID name database](https://github.com/zecoxao/sce_symbols/blob/2883963a0a514ba6e77407a08ef4730a65e88254/aerolib.csv) names their NIDs, and the NID of each name matches: the 83 `FTA_*` module and format helpers, `FT_Add_Module`, `FT_Alloc`, `FT_Attach_Stream`, `FT_CMap_Done`, `FT_CMap_New`, `FT_Done_GlyphSlot`, `FT_Done_Memory`, `FT_Free` and `FT_Get_Module_Interface`. Each helper is named after its symbol and keeps the explicit inventory alias. Their prototypes and behavior are unverified; each uses the no-parameter import-resolution convention and throws through `NotImplemented_nid_no_patch`, and the separate libSceFreeType implementations do not supply this provider. These declarations make no ABI or behavior claim.
