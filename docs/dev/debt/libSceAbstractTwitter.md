# libSceAbstractTwitter technical debt

### Known export names; ABI prototypes unverified

- 52 pending exports in [Export.cpp](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAbstractTwitter/Export.cpp) are present in the [PS5-3.20_Libs](https://github.com/DNNDHH/PS5-3.20_Libs) inventory. They are named after the mangled symbols the [verified NID name database](https://github.com/zecoxao/sce_symbols/blob/2883963a0a514ba6e77407a08ef4730a65e88254/aerolib.csv) gives for their NIDs, and the NID of each name matches. Their prototypes and behavior are unverified. They use the no-parameter import-resolution convention and throw through `NotImplemented_nid_no_patch`; these declarations make no ABI or behavior claim.
- [Constant answers](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAbstractTwitter/Export.cpp) (libSceAbstractTwitter) - 16 `TwitterContent`, `TwitterFolder`, `TwitterService` and `TwitterStorage` methods return a constant whatever their arguments: `0x809E0005` from `GetStat`, `Read`, `SeekTime`, `Flush`, `Remove`, `Open`, `Close` and `GetStorageSize`, 3 from `GetService` and `GetServiceType`, `0x6C0A` from `TwitterContent::GetCapability`; only the 32-bit result is claimed, and the declarations keep the no-parameter convention. In firmware 13.42's `libSceAbstractTwitter.sprx` (SHA-256 `e851cc7a05f7d7451f89bc0d73b139257370d95010ba90275f00c9fcd705d228`), read from disassembly, each body is `mov $<value>,%eax; ret`. A payload process on a PS5 Pro (firmware 13.60) loaded the module with `dlopen` alone and returned exactly these constants from every method that resolves by name; `TwitterFolder::RemoveFolder`, `TwitterFolder::RemoveContent` and `TwitterStorage::RemoveRecursive` did not resolve by name in this firmware, so their answers are not confirmed. The name of the error, what 3 and `0x6C0A` stand for and the result types remain unverified.
